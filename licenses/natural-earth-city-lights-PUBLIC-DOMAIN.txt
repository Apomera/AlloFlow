Selection: the 99 records with SCALERANK <= 2. City names and longitude/latitude are retained; coordinates are rounded to four decimal degrees. Brightness and display light spread are authored visual choices, not satellite radiance data or population estimates. The selection is illustrative and is not a reconstruction of 1969 illumination.
