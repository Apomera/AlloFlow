# Cephalopod Hunter visual enhancement, pass thirteen

Six benthic octopuses receive curved muted irises, dark domed pupils and skin lids in place of the old gold/blue eye buttons. The giant Pacific octopus also receives a scale-aware resting height so its larger arms and suckers clear the sand. Nearby reef rocks gain irregular mineral relief and bounded roughness variation under the existing lighting.

The animal factory preserves every non-eye mesh and pose. Humboldt squid, nautilus and the four previously upgraded swimming rigs remain exact. Each affected eye pair still uses eight meshes, with 2,352 fewer triangles and 1,360 fewer vertices. Eyes remain static geometry, without new textures, dive RNG or animated glare. The iris colors and fixed horizontal apertures are illustrative; pupil dilation and independent eye motion are outside this change. See [model notes](visual-notes.md) for anatomy references and attachment checks.

The rock material preserves all 44 seeded boulders, 354 construction RNG draws, geometry, shadows, substrate metadata, collision and camouflage logic. Coral, sand, plants and water effects retain their existing shaders. An initial periodic grain was rejected after reviewing its woven appearance; the accepted candidate uses two smooth 3D noise samples with grain that fades at distance. Slope is capped at 0.12 and roughness at 0.86–0.98. Unsupported derivative contexts retain the previous rock material. See [environment notes](environment-notes.md) for the refinement and exact arithmetic cost. No frame-rate improvement is claimed.

The giant-only resting height changes from 0.55 to 0.77 metres. Spawn, stored height, floor clamp, propulsion clearance and floor-relative camouflage use the same local reference. Other species retain 0.55 metres, and the shared constant, animal geometry, swimming limits and collision solver remain unchanged. Sampling 112 poses and 2,036,608 visible body/sucker vertices changed minimum analytic terrain clearance from −0.20274 to +0.01726 metres at the reef origin. This does not promise complete contact on arbitrary steep slopes.

## Validation

Final results and runtime hashes are recorded in [validation-summary.json](validation-summary.json): 148 distinct unit cases and both low/balanced browser cases passed. The first broad unit run passed 135 assertions but exited with two worker-startup errors; the same two files passed all nine assertions in a separate run with unchanged settings. Final rock and giant-grounding checks ran after the visual refinements. The new grounding suite initially failed during source-expression extraction; a test-only parser correction retained its assertions and all three cases then passed. Low and balanced browser checks use actual submitted eye and rock programs, validate native compilation/linking, stable resources, pause/reduced motion/resume and disposal. Video and trace capture remain disabled; this is functional validation rather than a performance benchmark.

[visual-review.cjs](visual-review.cjs) reproduces the six ordinary paused inspections, the 390×844 phone view and a camera-only boulder detail. All eight final images were reviewed and accepted. Final giant inspection checks all 1,512 rendered arm vertices against the actual floor's triangles; the minimum clearance was 0.02883 metres. [rock-detail-fixture.json](rock-detail-fixture.json) pins the real boulder's identity and transform. The detail camera changes no material, lighting, visibility or geometry. `--initial` additionally requires the ignored local pre-change source snapshot; final captures work from the checkout.

Unrelated shared work is preserved. Automatic approval initially rejected direct mirror copies; read-only checks proved the mirrors matched the saved baseline, and guarded enhancement hunks were applied instead.
