Where this fits

Geology Explorer is one of the tools in the AlloFlow STEM Lab. It sits in the Earth & Space Science area of the STEM Lab. AlloFlow is free and open source, and it runs in a browser.

Not yet. AlloFlow has written manuals for a few tools so far — see the manuals and guides page — and this one is not among them. The tool itself carries its instructions on screen.
