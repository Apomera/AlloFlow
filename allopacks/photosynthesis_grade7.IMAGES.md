# Image shot-list — Photosynthesis (Grade 7)

> **Text-free policy (applies to every prompt below).** Artwork must contain **no raster text,
> labels, numbers, captions or watermarks**. Where a prompt below asks for a label or a caption,
> that text belongs in AlloFlow's native label and caption fields, anchored over the image, not
> drawn into it. Icons, arrows and diagram marks are fine as long as they carry no characters.
> Alt text describes the rendered artwork and is reviewed against it before shipping.
>
> This list uses the earlier short format. `day_night_sky_grade1.IMAGES.md` shows the fuller one
> (policy, a glossary picture table, grouped lesson panels, alt-text rules) if a richer pass is
> wanted for this pack.


Generate in-app (Image tool) or any generator; attach to the noted resource. Alt text is
written to born-accessible standards — use it verbatim.

## 1. `ph-img-leaf-cross-section` — attach to "The Sugar Factory in Every Leaf" (ph-reading)
**Prompt:** A clean labeled educational cross-section of a leaf for middle schoolers: upper
surface, palisade cells containing green chloroplasts, a vein carrying water, and a stoma on
the underside with arrows showing carbon dioxide entering and oxygen leaving. Flat colorful
illustration, large clear labels, no photorealism.
**Alt text:** "Cross-section diagram of a leaf. Green chloroplasts fill the cells inside. A
vein carries water in from the stem. On the underside, an opening labeled stoma has one arrow
showing carbon dioxide entering and another showing oxygen leaving."

## 2. `ph-img-van-helmont` — optional, attach to the quiz or FAQ
**Prompt:** A simple two-panel historical illustration: left panel shows a small willow
sapling in a pot of soil being weighed; right panel shows the same tree five years later, much
larger, with the soil weighed again and nearly unchanged. Educational storybook style.
**Alt text:** "Two panels. Left: a small willow in a pot of soil on a balance scale. Right:
the same willow five years later, far larger, while the soil on the scale weighs almost the
same as before."

## 3. `ph-img-equation-strip` — optional, attach to the Inputs and Outputs Map (ph-outline)
**Prompt:** A horizontal visual equation strip for kids: sun icon plus water drop plus carbon
dioxide molecule, an arrow, then a sugar cube icon plus oxygen molecule. Bright, iconic, few
words.
**Alt text:** "Visual equation: sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide on the left; an arrow
pointing right to glucose and oxygen."
