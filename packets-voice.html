Make Word Sounds practice
This page uses an AI voice, on this device. No account. Your words are not uploaded. See the details Hide the details
  • This is one of the two Packets pages that use AI. The other is Make a read-along. The other pages do not, and nothing on them changes.
  • The voice is Kokoro, a computer voice made with AI. It runs inside this browser tab. The words you type are turned into sound on this device and are not sent anywhere.
  • Nothing is downloaded until you press Get the voice. Then the page downloads the voice, about 88 MB, one time, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Hugging Face can see that your browser asked for the voice. It does not receive your words.
  • The program that runs the voice comes from this site (or AlloFlow's CDN at alloflow-cdn.pages.dev when hosted on a mirror without the engine files), and the page checks it before running it.
  • Each recording is kept in this browser's storage for this site, under My words, marked as a computer voice. It is not uploaded. It goes only into the files you make.
  • There are no cookies and nothing to sign in to.

The rule this page gives your browser. The browser refuses anything the rule does not allow: It is the Content-Security-Policy line near the top of this page's source.

Make recordings with a computer voice

For a word that has no recording. Each recording you make here is kept in My words on this device, and the Word Sounds maker and Present or export then use it for that word.

This browser is not keeping your words, so a recording made here could not be kept. Try a window that is not private.

Step 1

Your words

One on each line, or with spaces or commas between them. Letters only, 2 to 24 letters each. At most 60 words at a time. The list below says which words already have a recording.

    Step 2

    The voice

    The word recordings that come with Packets were made with a Kokoro voice too.

    Downloaded one time from Hugging Face, then kept by this browser. On a slow connection this takes a few minutes.

    Step 3

    Meanings from the vocabulary maker

    They have no recording yet. They are said with the voice of the meaning recordings that come with Packets (Heart), whatever voice is chosen above, so every answer of a question sounds the same. Each recording is kept in My words on this device, and the vocabulary maker uses it by itself.

      Step 3

      Make the recordings

      Listen to each one. A computer voice can say a word wrong: make it again, or record the word yourself in the maker with Edit this word.

      Get the voice in step 2 first.

      When you are done: Make Word Sounds practice or Present & print word cards (there, choose My saved words under From this browser). To remove a recording later, use My words on this device in the maker, or Remove here.
