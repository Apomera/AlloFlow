# Machine Lab: saved calculation cards

Saved power and motion practice now appears as named cards with a prominent calculated result, a separate entered answer, saved assumptions and visible checked-answer or viewed-solution provenance. Solid and dashed status borders complement the text labels. A native disclosure on each card expands the complete original saved setup and working.

The result and unit are recomputed from the saved record, even when the live task changes or becomes invalid. Entered answers can be blank, unfinished or arbitrary text in viewed solutions; they remain clearly separate from the calculated result. Hint counts and the full practice-not-score explanation remain visible.

Cards stay in a single vertical collection. Result and entered-answer panels sit side by side on desktop and stack on narrow screens. Text wraps, numeric values use tabular figures, and forced colors preserve card and disclosure boundaries.

Only the new saved-practice renderer and its call changed. Existing physics, grading, record validation, handlers, saved state and work-record exports remain unchanged. The expandable snapshot text is generated by the original, unchanged `powerRecordText()`.
