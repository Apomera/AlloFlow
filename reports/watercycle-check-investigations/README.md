# Water Cycle: investigate an answer, then reconsider

Check yourself now gives questions and explanations their own readable space. After answering, learners can open a relevant process model or native condition control, record an observation or model limit, and reconsider their answer. Reflections keep the original question and first answer and are unscored.

## What changed

- Full-width questions, readable submitted choices, one quiet progress line, and native actions with at least 44px targets.
- Investigations for all ten myths and the authored quiz concepts, with explicit prompts and model boundaries. Related models identify quantities and processes the tool does not calculate.
- Read-only water-state diagrams and a groundwater pore-space schematic. Temperature, wind and land-cover actions open existing controls without changing their values.
- Return focuses the preserved question and places reconsideration beside its feedback. Writing is kept as it is typed; native reflection history retains drafts and saved work across new questions.
- First-answer scoring and vocabulary rewards retain their original rules. Navigation preserves the mounted canvas, paused parcel, saved comparison, evidence and writing.

## Validation

**311 distinct tests passed across 12 files:** 55 new Check tests, 16 existing accessibility/content tests, and 240 focused regressions. **1666 browser checks**, **294 snapshots**, **208 recorded native Enter activations**, and **22 scoped accessibility audits** passed with zero violations. Final captures and run reports are pinned to the tested source hash.

The browser run covers eight panel/theme configurations, including 320px phones, narrow embedded tools, dark mode and forced colors. It exercises destination mappings, native temperature and wind changes, first answers and rewards, study-term focus, reflection writing/save/history, long choices, empty and changed questions, and the paused 3D aquifer at progress 0.42.

Source comparison preserves 44 model, state and bank anchors, both original scored callback statement sets, and the full comparison and signal derivations. Source and public runtime copies match, and the existing preview serves that version.
