SwimLab

How swimming works (stroke physics + survival skills) plus what every swimmer should know about cold water, rip currents, ice, life jackets, and rescue. Visual stroke breakdowns, the science of buoyancy and propulsion, and the survival skills (back float, eggbeater, HELP, huddle) that actually save lives. Sources cited
