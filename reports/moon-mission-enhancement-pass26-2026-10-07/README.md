**Moon Mission: dust impacts on cratered terrain**

Implemented locally on October 8, 2026; started October 7. No commit or staging command was created by this agent.

Dust in the 3D descent scene now follows a ballistic path until its first intersection with the actual terrain triangles. Grains stop early on rising ground and remain airborne longer over hollows. Each short streak splits at terrain face boundaries so its straight segments stay above the surface. The same immutable emission records drive the scene after pause, camera changes and graphics rebuilding.

The solver computes contact times once per frozen birth and terrain sampler. It uses local lunar gravity and the existing scene scale. The particle pool stays bounded; each grain draws at most 16 trail segments. Queries reaching the finite terrain boundary retire there. A 2.5-second display horizon also retires grains that have not hit terrain. Exceptionally dense terrain crossings shorten a trail within its latest 0.07 seconds while preserving its head. The model note explains the display horizon and the different ground model used by the 2D view.
