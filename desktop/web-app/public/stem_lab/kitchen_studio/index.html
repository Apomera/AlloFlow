    Your kitchen, in words

    The sink and prep board are on the left. The hob and oven are in the center. The measuring jug and refrigerator are on the right. Every station and action is available in the controls below.

    Try it with your hands

    Tap a carry tool to pick it up, then tap its destination to place it. Dragging also works. Other tools use their shown gestures. Escape or Cancel carried tool cancels the preview.

    Use the 3D tools with keyboard

    Choose a tool and pick it up. Arrow keys move it; Shift moves farther. For smaller steps, select Fine movement before pickup or press F while holding a tool. T aims at the next available target. Enter or Space releases; Escape cancels. Tab leaves the interaction.

    Choose a tool to begin.

    Tool feedback (optional)

    Gentle cues mark picking up a tool, reaching a release position, and putting it down. The burner dial clicks as its displayed setting changes. Choose sounds, touch pulses where supported, or both.

    Tool sounds off. Touch pulses off.

    Rehearse a full handwash

    Rub the model hands side to side three times, then move them under the tap and release. These short gestures stand in for the full washing and drying routine.

    In a real kitchen, scrub with soap for at least 20 seconds, then rinse and dry well. Handwashing guidance · CDC
