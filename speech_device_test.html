Speech Speed Check

Measures how fast this device can turn speech into text without sending audio anywhere, and whether this network allows the downloads.

Before you start

The test plays no sound and never uses the microphone. It transcribes a short public-domain recording that is built into this page. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Close other tabs and plug the device in.
  • Allow about 5 to 15 minutes. Keep this tab open and on screen.
  • It downloads speech models from the internet: up to about 140 MB, or about 450 MB with the larger sizes.

Use the model only. Please don't enter a person's or a school's name.
