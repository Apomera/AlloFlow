# AlloFlow school data flow inventory

October 24, 2026. This inventory describes the reviewed application paths for the Gemini Canvas and Portland Public Schools, Maine assessment. A district deployment must select its enabled features and identify the actual operator, account, endpoints, storage, and retention for those paths. The presence of a feature in the source does not mean PPS has approved or enabled it.

AlloFlow uses local storage, peer connections, and optional remote services. It has no application-wide guarantee that student information stays off servers, that all saved work is encrypted, or that ending a session deletes every copy. Codenames and random browser identifiers can reduce direct identification but do not make contextual records anonymous.

## Canvas service boundary

The [managed request follow-up](managed-request-security-followup.md) binds reviewed requests to captured routes, keys, models and approval through native retries/fallbacks. Configured managed deployments now block the legacy web/video URL importer, including public CORS relays and direct Gemini import. Search approval alone does not approve those source routes; use manual paste/local files while an approved route is unavailable. Source URLs and provider bodies are omitted from importer diagnostics. An absent/null managed policy retains unmanaged behavior.

Google documents enterprise protections for eligible Education users of the Gemini app. Its separate Canvas guidance warns that shared multi-user app data can be viewed or edited through a public app link and cautions against entering sensitive information. Those statements require a workflow-specific review; they do not prove that a particular AlloFlow record was exposed. [Education FAQ](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/workspace-with-gemini/gemini-for-google-workspace-faq-education), [Canvas safety guidance](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16419134?hl=en).

For the PPS proposal, use public or synthetic material in Canvas until the approved feature scope, account restrictions, app sharing, API routes, storage bridge, backend access, and deletion authority are established. Keep real clinical mode and clinical AI at their default blocked settings while those approvals are unresolved. A policy flag documents a configuration choice; it is not evidence of district approval or a server-side staff identity check.

## Retention and deletion responsibilities

Reviewed deletion is now available for the complete protected clinical store. A separate dry-run-first operator tool covers selected expired assignments in an owned Firebase project, including dependent records, and the revision-26 mailbox has an absolute live-access deadline plus a scoped manual Drive review workflow. These controls do not select one clinical student, configure a Canvas-provisioned backend, delete every Drive/provider copy, or set a district age-based records schedule. See the [retention and deletion implementation](retention-deletion-followup.md) for exact scope, safeguards and verified evidence.

The data owner should assign a retention period, legal-hold exception, deletion authority, and verification method to every enabled row above. Record separate outcomes for local/browser storage, bridge storage, encrypted recovery, the active backend, Drive, exports, and provider copies. A request to delete one student or case can span several locations; a generic browser-clear instruction cannot establish completion across them.

Firestore TTL is asynchronous, and a parent TTL deletion does not delete its subcollections. Expiry, access denial, and deletion are separate controls. Confirm the deployed TTL policies and recursive/orphan cleanup rather than treating an `expiresAt` field or a client session-end action as a deletion receipt. [Firestore TTL behavior](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/ttl).

Other optional school services, including Educator Evaluation, School Rewards/Print Lab, or externally integrated tools, need their own approved service/account and records schedule. Their data is not covered merely by approving the live-session rows or the protected clinical store. Use the [district audit](report.md) to track those separate scopes and deployment evidence.

## District configuration record

For each enabled row, record the release, feature setting, operator, account/project, recipient endpoint, data categories, permitted purpose, agreement, verified access controls, retention/holds, deletion owner, and restore/offboarding result. The district must approve these actual choices; this inventory supplies no promise of FERPA, COPPA, Maine-law, or other legal compliance by architecture.
