This page runs without AI. No account. The words you choose are not uploaded. See the details Hide the details
  • Nothing is generated when you make a file or when a student uses it. The spoken sounds were recorded ahead of time with two AI voices (Kokoro and Google Gemini).
  • The practice file is put together in this browser tab. The page looks your words up in a word list and a sound dictionary that it has already loaded. Your words and your choices are not sent anywhere.
  • There are no cookies and nothing to sign in to.
  • The page loads files from this site only, including the part that makes recordings smaller (MP3). At step 3 it gets the sound files, also from this site. Most are the same files whatever you choose. Some words have their sounds in small files of about 40 words each, and the page gets only the ones your words need.
  • Like any website, the server that sends these files can see that your browser asked for them. That includes which of the small sound files were asked for, but not which words. It receives nothing else.
  • On this device the page keeps your last choices and the sound files. The buttons that remove them are under About this page, at the bottom.
  • What you add to a word (its sounds, your recording, your picture) is kept in this browser's storage for this site. It is not uploaded. It goes only into the files you make. You can remove it under My words on this device, in step 2.
  • The microphone is used only when you press Record it yourself, and only until the recording stops. The recording stays on this device.
  • This page reaches only this site. One other page, Find a picture, can search Global Symbols, Openverse and Wikimedia Commons after you turn its search on there. It sends them the word you search for, and asks them for the pictures it shows.

Every file this page loads: they are listed in the page's source, and all of them are on this site.

    How to choose the words
    Each level keeps to the letter patterns taught up to it. sh, ch and th start at Level 3. Choose 'Any level' to use them earlier. For spelling, choose a level: your students then spell only patterns they have been taught.
    Levels to mix Choose two or more levels for a balanced random mix across them.

    About these levels

    Answer choices (Step 1)

    • Easy: 3 choices in Find Sounds, Blend Sounds and Rhyme Time; Find Sounds asks the first sound; Sound Counting shows the count with two numbers either side; Break it Down shows the word's sounds and one more; Sight & Spell shows a smaller letter bank and word length.
    • Medium: 4 choices in Find Sounds and Rhyme Time, 6 in Blend Sounds; Find Sounds asks the first or last sound; Sound Counting shows 1 to 8.
    • All: every choice, any sound, and 1 to 11 or more.

            Saved. What to do next

            1. Send the file to your students the way you send any file: your learning platform, a shared drive or a USB drive. From Google Drive or Google Classroom, a student downloads the file first: the preview there does not run it.
            2. Each student opens it in a browser, finishes it and presses the main button at the end, Save my work. They hand that file back to you.
            3. Add the results up on Add up results, or open them in a spreadsheet. If your school uses AlloFlow, its Class Analytics reads these results files too.

            If the file does not open on a device (a phone, a tablet, or a school computer that will not open downloaded files), a student opens it on Open a practice file or check: apomera.github.io/AlloFlow/packets-open.html.

              About this page

              What this page keeps

              Your last choices are kept in this browser so that the page opens the way you left it: the activities, how you chose the words, any words you typed or checked in the list, the version, the length, which letters Letter Trace traces, whether to show letter hints and how many choices to show. The sound files are kept too, so that they are not downloaded again. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

              What you add to a word (its sounds, your recording, your picture) is kept in this browser's storage for this site, until you remove it under My words on this device, in step 2. It is not uploaded.

              Where the words and sounds come from

              • The spoken sounds were recorded ahead of time with two AI voices (Kokoro and Google Gemini). A word you record yourself in step 2 uses your recording. Each practice file lists its credits under About this file.
