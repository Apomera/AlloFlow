# Conch shelter visual candidate

This candidate replaces only `makeConchMesh` and inserts the adjacent `createCLHuntConchGeometry(T)` helper in `stem_lab/stem_tool_cephalopodlab.js`. It changes the stack of cones, rings and partial sphere into one continuous indexed shell surface with a tapered nonplanar spiral, restrained sculpted ribs, a rolled flared lip and a genuinely recessed opening. The opening sits on the right of the shell and faces mostly forward in the unchanged shelter Group frame.

## References and limits

[NOAA Fisheries: Queen Conch](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/queen-conch) describes the heavy whorl-shaped shell, short spines near the apex and pink interior lip. Its photographs provide a reference for the contrast between the weathered exterior and warmer aperture. [NOAA's queen conch consultation guidance](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/s3/2025-08/Queen-conch-consultation-guidance.pdf) identifies the flared, thickening lip as an adult characteristic. These support the visual cues used here; the simulator still labels the object generically as a conch shelter.

This is a stylized empty shelter, inspired by a mature conch shell. The swept three-turn surface is a bounded visual approximation rather than a scanned specimen or an exact growth model. Low rounded shoulder relief replaces sharp species-specific spines. The visible cavity is a shallow connected recess, not a complete internal spiral or a model of a living gastropod. Exterior mineral tones and the darker interior are fixed vertex colors; no actual animal, operculum or soft tissue is implied.

## Geometry and resource contract

- One direct mesh, `cl-conch-shell`, with one independently owned opaque `MeshStandardMaterial`, `cl-conch-material`. White material color, vertex colors enabled, roughness 0.58 and the default front-face rendering. No material callback, shader, texture, light or per-frame attribute update.
- Root validated the actual topology: 1,682 vertices, 3,360 triangles and 80,712 bytes for indexed position, normal and color buffers. The strict guard confirmed these totals against the limits of 2,200 vertices and 3,400 triangles.
- Position, normal and color are the only attributes. Every actor owns separate geometry, arrays and material; no shared cache alters the existing disposal traversal.
- Fifty cross-sections with 28 vertices each form a tapered helical exterior. The radial tube dimension stays smaller than the final body coil radius; height remains near the original pointed shell silhouette. The rolled lip has six connected cross-sections and the cavity has four connected cross-sections and a recessed closing pole.
- The vertical centerline uses a smooth quadratic descent with a finite initial slope. Root's first strict guard identified a real lower-spire sweep fold at triangle 61 when the original vertical profile had excessive initial curvature. The profile was refined to remove that fold while retaining both height endpoints, all ring counts and the strict triangle/normal assertion. The author has not executed the refined candidate.
- Root's next strict guard identified a sharp rolled-lip transition at triangle 2,885. Three intermediate cross-sections were added around the actual lip crest, distributing its turn from exterior wall through the outward cap to the inner wall. The shell remains one physically joined indexed surface; no split normals or weakened checks hide the transition. The outer/inner lip metadata now identifies the crest and final inner rim of this six-ring profile.
- Semantic parts are `spire-outer`, `body-whorl`, `flared-lip` and `aperture-recess`. Index ranges are contiguous. A part's vertex span describes vertices newly allocated by that part; its triangles may reference the preceding part's shared joining ring. Joint triangles use the same actual vertex indices, not overlapping duplicates.

`geometry.userData.clConchParts` contains vertex and triangle/index ranges. `geometry.userData.clConchGeometry` locates the actual exterior section rings, shared spire/body ring, shell/lip ring, outer and inner rolled lip rings, four cavity section rings, aperture axis/center, recessed pole and tip. The supplied aperture ray is a locator for testing actual triangles, not a substitute for physical geometry checks. Its origin is 0.20 units beyond the mouth, directed into the recess; a ray must travel at least another 0.06 units after crossing the mouth plane before finding the interior.

## Protected behavior

The guard replaces the complete original conch constructor once, then proves reversing that one replacement restores every source byte. Disjoint sponge or prompt changes do not invalidate that proof. It does not modify other shelter factories, `SHELTER_TYPES`, `makeShelterMesh`, `spawnShelter`, initial shelter distribution, terrain placement, hermit shell rewards, the carrying loop or any other actor.

The shelter remains the same unscaled Group frame. Gameplay never indexes its children. `spawnShelter` still supplies its original position, `shelterType`, state, `createdAt` and one random wobble phase. Carrying still reparents the Group to the animal at `(0, -0.1, 0.6)` and applies the existing rotation wobble. Dropping still returns the Group to the scene at the player's horizontal position and `y = 0.32`, resets its rotation and starts the existing lifetime. The conch retains its 0.40 camouflage bonus, 0.15 carrying penalty, 60,000 ms dropped lifetime, pickup and den radii, announcement and reward behavior. Existing traversal disposes the one geometry and one material once when the dropped shelter expires or the scene is cleaned up.
