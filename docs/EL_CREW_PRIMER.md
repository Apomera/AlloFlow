# Crew, HOWLs, and EL Education: a one-page primer for teachers new to the words

Written 2026-09-13 for the King Middle School PD series. AlloFlow's Crew Launch packs and two SEL
Hub tools (HOWL Tracker, Crew Protocols) use EL Education's vocabulary. This page says what the
words mean and where to read more. The same text, shortened, appears inside the packs' FAQ and in
both tools under "New to Crew or HOWLs?".

## The words

**EL Education** (formerly Expeditionary Learning) is a national school-improvement model that grew
out of a partnership between Outward Bound and Harvard's Graduate School of Education in the early
1990s. EL schools organise learning around long interdisciplinary projects ("learning
expeditions"), assess character alongside academics, and put a daily community structure called
Crew at the centre of school culture. King Middle School was one of the first ten EL schools (1992)
and has been an EL Education Credentialed School with Mentor School status since 2016.

**Crew** is EL's word for advisory. In a secondary school, a Crew is a small group of students and
one adult (the Crew leader) who meet regularly, in King's case daily, to check in, set and review
goals, build community, and work through what is hard. The phrase behind it is Kurt Hahn's "We are
crew, not passengers." In elementary EL schools, Crew is the whole class and the morning meeting.

**HOWLs** are Habits of Work and Learning: the character traits an EL school teaches and assesses
separately from academic content. King names three, each with a student-voiced statement on its
grading guide:

| HOWL | Statement |
| --- | --- |
| Respect | "I am a respectful member of the King community." |
| Responsibility | "I take responsibility for my success as a learner." |
| Perseverance | "I persevere to produce high quality work." |

HOWLs are graded on progress reports and trimester report cards, one grade per HOWL per class,
apart from academic grades. Other EL schools name four (Active Engagement, Effective Effort, Crew
Membership, Habits of Mind) and some call them Habits of Scholarship; the HOWL Tracker tool carries
both sets.

**Learning targets** are EL's student-friendly goal statements ("I can…"). **Expeditions** are the
8-to-12-week projects. **Protocols** are the structured discussion and reflection formats Crew and
classes use (circles, fishbowls, tuning protocols), many of them from the National School Reform
Faculty and the School Reform Initiative.

linked as the organisation's own pages on the topic.

## How AlloFlow uses the words

- The six **Crew Launch packs** (`allopacks/crew_*_grade6_8`) are one 15-minute Crew slot each,
  weeks 1 to 6: norms, repair, zones, one system, asking, feedback. Each carries a Crew station and
  links to SEL Hub tools; each FAQ ends with "How does this connect to my HOWL grade?" and, from
  tonight, opens with "What is Crew?" and "What is a HOWL?".
- **HOWL Tracker** has a preset for King's three HOWLs with the grading-guide statements, a weekly
  self-rating with evidence, and the Crew station record as evidence.
- **Crew Protocols** is the library of circle, opening, closing and repair formats, with sources.
