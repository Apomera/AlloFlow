# Crew Launch Week 5: Asking for What You Need - image shot list (text-free policy)

Companion to `crew_ask_grade6_8.allopack.json`. The pack ships text-only and renders fully without images. Illustrations, when generated, go into a separate `allopacks/illustrated/` edition with WebP assets under `allopacks/media/crew_ask_grade6_8/`.

Policy: **no raster text, labels, numbers, captions or watermarks in the artwork.** Speech bubbles stay empty or carry a simple icon. Labels and captions live in AlloFlow's native fields. Alt text describes the final artwork and is reviewed against it before shipping. Style: warm, simple, flat; a diverse middle-school Crew of about ten students and one adult; no logos, no school names, no faces of real people.

## Glossary pictures (square, 480 px, one per term)

| slot | term | generator prompt (flat, no text) | must show / must avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| ca-term-advocate | Advocate | A student standing at a teacher's desk, calm posture, one hand slightly raised | not pleading |
| ca-term-describe | Describe | A magnifying glass over a worksheet with plain crossed-out marks | no numbers legible |
| ca-term-express | Express | A simple heart outline with a small wavy line inside | plain symbol |
| ca-term-ask | Ask | An open hand, palm up, at desk height | small, specific gesture |
| ca-term-reinforce | Reinforce | Two arrows from opposite sides meeting at a small circle | plain symbol |
| ca-term-vague | Vague | A fogged-over speech bubble, empty | bubble empty |

## Lesson panels (900 px wide; each carries native labels and a caption)

1. `ca-img-stuck` - *Three days stuck.* A student at a desk with an eraser worn down, a page of half-erased work, a calendar with three days shaded. Labels: Erasing, Guessing. Caption: the question stays inside and the assignment gets harder every day.
2. `ca-img-fourmoves` - *The four-sentence ask.* The same student at the teacher's desk, four empty speech bubbles in a vertical row with the four icons (magnifier, heart, open hand, meeting arrows). Labels: Describe, Express, Ask, Reinforce. Caption: ninety seconds; the teacher knew exactly what to do.
3. `ca-img-options` - *Options after a no.* Four small doors in a row with icons: a speech bubble, a second person, a pencil and envelope, a clock. Labels: A different way, A different person, In writing, A time to come back. Caption: giving up is the only move not on the list.
