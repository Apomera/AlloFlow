Build, observe, calculate, and explain.

Geometry World uses the familiar building and exploration of block-based 3D sandbox games to make geometric measurement visible. Count cubes in a layer, connect layers to multiplication, and compare strategies. Explaining and checking those calculations provides practice toward accurate, flexible calculation fluency.

Use your geometry knowledge to design and measure rooms. Apply volume to real-world architecture!

Lesson goals

  1. Build a room with walls 4 blocks high
  2. Calculate the interior volume of your room
  3. Add a second room connected to the first
  4. Find the total volume of both rooms combined

Your lesson route

  1. Architect · investigation
  2. Room Inspector · 2 question steps
  3. Volume Coach · 3 question steps
