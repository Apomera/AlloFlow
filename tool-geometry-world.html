Geometry World

Build and explore in a block-based 3D sandbox. Connect cubes and layers to volume, practice calculation strategies, and explain your reasoning on matching worksheets.

Open Geometry World Opens the tool directly — no account, no signup, nothing to install.

Share this tool with a class or a colleague

This link opens Geometry World on its own. Paste it into an assignment, a sub plan, a slide, or a message and it opens on the tool the student needs.

Where this fits

Geometry World is one of the tools in the AlloFlow STEM Lab. It sits in the Geometry & Measurement area of the STEM Lab. AlloFlow is free and open source, and it runs in a browser.

See every STEM tool with a shareable link, or search the tool finder by topic or classroom goal.

Is there a manual for this tool?

Not yet. AlloFlow has written manuals for a few tools so far — see the manuals and guides page — and this one is not among them. The tool itself carries its instructions on screen.
