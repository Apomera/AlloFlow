# Aquaculture Lab measurement recovery

Completed locally on 2026-10-03. Changes remain unstaged and uncommitted. No push, deployment, full app or installer build.

## What changed

Removing a live captured reading now keeps it under **Recently removed readings**. Up to the last eight removed canonical readings are held in this page, with separate live-notebook and removed-reading counts. Unique session keys distinguish recovery entries. Restore returns the original reading at its removal-time index clamped to the current notebook length; current rows keep their order. This does not promise complete historical ordering after later edits.

Each removed reading retains its captured source, raw A/B values, original labels and units, date, model, events and full A/B settings. Zero, no-A and A-only readings remain distinct. Restoration never reruns the model, regenerates a capture or replaces a newer row. Full notebooks, conflicting IDs and exact canonical-signature duplicates keep the removed original available. **Review matching reading**, **Review current reading** and **Review notebook space** open and focus the existing live notebook destinations. Making space by removing another reading no longer loses the earlier removed reading.

Native disclosures and described, wrapping buttons support keyboard use and narrow screens. Recovery availability is ordinary per-row text. Deliberate actions announce their outcome once. A successful restore focuses the original live reading heading after React renders it; archived copies cannot take that focus.

Loading a guided question, branching from a saved design, replaying a saved investigation, returning to the parked draft, or successfully importing a backup clears session recovery after the action's guards pass. Rejected or blocked transitions keep it. Same-notebook design and A edits, presets, builder reset and evidence save keep it. Reloading or closing the page ends recovery. Removed entries are not persisted, archived or included in backups; this pass adds no archive schema or persistent trash.

Restore keeps later writing, other live readings, saved investigations and parked drafts intact. Device-save failure keeps the restored reading and current work in this page, subject to the existing save-recovery controls. Removed entries remain session-only even when a backup is downloaded. The original capture and Evidence append logic are preserved. Physiology, frozen-measurement construction and model version `2026.10-stock-feedback` are unchanged.
