# Open RN mirror rights

Open Resources for Nursing (Open RN) publishes its grant materials under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license. AlloFlow mirrors the
five selected current second-edition textbooks from their structured NCBI
Bookshelf editions.

The importer verifies both the Open RN attribution and a CC BY 4.0 link on
every source chapter. It copies instructional text only. Figures, media, and
site interface elements are removed because individual figures can identify
different sources or licenses. Each mirrored page retains its canonical NCBI
URL, word count, and SHA-256 digest.

- Open RN project and license: https://www.cvtc.edu/grants/open-rn
- NCBI Bookshelf: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

These are educational nursing resources, not a substitute for current clinical
policy, instructor guidance, or professional judgment in patient care.
