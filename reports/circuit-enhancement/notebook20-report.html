Explanation using evidence

The prediction was supported: lamp current rose from 3 mA to 8 mA. Only input voltage changed; the 5 V supply, 10 kΩ base resistor, and 220 Ω lamp stayed fixed. The supply provides the lamp energy.

Model and limits

Generic, fixed-wiring NPN DC model: VBE = 0.70 V when conducting; VCE saturation = 0.20 V; constant adjustable gain. The lamp has fixed resistance. IB = max(0, (Vth − 0.70)/(Rth + RB)); IC = min(β × IB, (VCC − 0.20)/Rlamp). Sensor dividers include base loading. Relative light maps to 100 kΩ × 100^(−level/100), from 100 kΩ to 1 kΩ; it is not a lux calibration. Temperature, switching transients, leakage, and breakdown are excluded.
