# Teamwork Retrospective: evidence, supported change and review

This pass replaces the three-card completion gate and repeatable save reward with an optional reflection cycle. Learners distinguish what happened from possible explanations, consider different accounts, propose one supported change and revisit its effects. Completing work, speaking in a meeting or writing a plan does not by itself demonstrate equitable collaboration.

Three authored fictional contexts address whose ideas influence decisions, hidden workload behind an on-time result, and silence during a public review. Each has elementary, middle and high-school setups, a process observation, a possible plan and a later outcome with a reasoned adjustment. A separate own-experience context supports transfer. Models are examples, not guaranteed solutions or a validated assessment.

Six optional notes cover evidence, perspectives, a change, shared responsibility, a review plan and subsequent revision. The three stages use native expandable panels. A later review may stay blank; hypothetical outcomes must be distinguished from actual observations. The activity allows discussion or drawing, passing on disclosure and appropriate adult support. It does not promise confidentiality or require someone affected by harm to resolve it in a group review.

The design draws on [CAST's monitoring-progress guidance](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/strategy-development/monitoring-progress/), which connects feedback to goals and supports reflection with prompts and examples, and [CAST's collective-reflection guidance](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/emotional-capacity/reflection/), which supports varied scaffolds and ways to understand progress. [The CASEL framework](https://files.casel.org/CASEL-SEL-Framework-11.2020.pdf) informs the emphasis on perspectives, collaborative problem-solving, support and consequences. These sources inform the design; they do not evaluate this activity.

Implementation and compatibility:

- `retroSelections[band]` remembers the context. `retroDrafts[band + ':' + contextId]` holds `evidence`, `perspectives`, `change`, `support`, `check` and `revision`. Fields remain editable and independent across contexts and grades. Unknown draft properties survive edits; malformed field values display as empty.
- The `retro` tab ID remains stable. Earlier card arrays, unfinished inputs, saved flags, reflections, logs and awards remain in project data. Recognized strings are shown in a separate historical panel, without automatically assigning them to a new context. Progress and badge descriptions identify earlier activity records.
- Reading, drafting and copying do not award XP, create a practice log, set the old saved flag or call an AI provider. Notes are part of the current project; the existing hub save/export controls preserve them beyond the session.
- A labeled, focusable, read-only text area previews only the current context's notes and marks missing fields. Copy uses the shared helper or native clipboard. False returns, rejected promises, synchronous errors and missing clipboard support produce an honest fallback message through the app toast and screen-reader announcer. The preview remains available for manual copying. Nothing is submitted to another person.
- Explicit light, dark and high-contrast colors, 16px inputs and controls at least 44px tall support phone use. Full context titles appear below native selectors where long option text may truncate. Shared worked examples can benefit from adult discussion for younger learners.

Validation results are recorded in `reports/sel-teamwork-retrospective/validation.json`. Accessibility evidence is scoped to the revised region, not every Teamwork tab or a full accessibility certification. No push, deployment or packaged build is included; ignored generated `desktop/app-build` output is not rebuilt.
