This page uses an AI voice, on this device. No account. Your passage is not uploaded. See the details Hide the details
  • This is one of the two Packets pages that use AI. The other is Make recordings with a computer voice. The other pages do not, and nothing on them changes.
  • The voice is Kokoro, a computer voice made with AI. It runs inside this browser tab. The passage you type is turned into sound on this device and is not sent anywhere.
  • The words are yours. This page does not write, shorten or change the passage.
  • Nothing is downloaded until you press Get the voice. Then the page downloads the voice, about 88 MB, one time, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Hugging Face can see that your browser asked for the voice. It does not receive your passage.
  • The program that runs the voice comes from this site (or AlloFlow's CDN at alloflow-cdn.pages.dev when hosted on a mirror without the engine files), and the page checks it before running it.
  • If you press Record on a sentence, this page asks for this device's microphone to record your voice inside this tab. Your recording stays on this device, is not stored and is not uploaded.
  • The passage and the recordings stay on this page only while it is open. They are not stored and not uploaded. They go only into the file you save.
  • This page loads its files from this site: the page itself, five stylesheets (packets.css, packets-make.css, packets-voice.css, packets-read-along.css, packets-read-along-print.css), ten scripts (packets-page.js, word_sounds_take_home_loader.js, packet_read_along_builder.js, syllable_table_en.js, packets_voice_engine.js, packets_verify_core.js, packets_reopen.js, packets_handoff.js, packets_zip.js, packets-read-along.js) and the tab icon. When you record or use the computer voice, it also loads packets_audio_kit.js and lame.min.js from this site to make recordings smaller. When you open Preview & customize worksheet or Worksheet audio player, it also loads packets-print-cards.css, packets-print-cards.js, and packets_docx.js from this site. When you tick Parts of speech, Word meanings or Offline word pictures in step 4, it also loads the word lists and picture sets for them from this site (packets_vocab_bank_en.js and its word lists, word_sounds_symbols_en.js, reading_symbols_ext_en.js, word_picture_semantic_en.js, word_picture_matcher.js, packets_my_words.js and the picture sets).
  • There are no cookies and nothing to sign in to.

The file you save can:

  • Show the passage a page at a time, and play your recordings of it, one sentence at a time, with the sentence highlighted.
  • Play one sentence again when the student presses it, and play more slowly.
  • Show longer words in syllables when the student presses Syllables. The breaks come from a word list on this site, looked up here when you make the file. Nothing is generated for them.
  • Offer the Reading tools every Packets file has: text size, colors, spacing, letters, a reading focus line and a magnifier.
  • Keep the student's place on that device.

The file you save cannot:

  • Run AI. It plays the recordings that were made here. It makes no speech of its own, unless you turn on the local device voice in step 4: then it can ask the student's device to read with a voice already installed on that device.
  • Connect to the internet. The file tells the browser to refuse every connection, and the browser enforces it.
  • Listen to the student, unless you turn on optional student voice practice in step 4. Then a recording stays in the memory of the student's browser and is never sent. It is saved only if the student presses Save my recording, and then only as a file on that device. The file never uses the camera.

The rule this page gives your browser. The browser refuses anything the rule does not allow: It is the Content-Security-Policy line near the top of this page's source.

Make a read-along

Type or paste a passage. A computer voice records it one sentence at a time, and you save one file that reads it to a student with each sentence highlighted as it is read. Listening practice: nothing is scored.

Open that page

Step 1

Your passage

The student sees it at the top of the file. Left empty, it is "Read along".
Up to 6,000 letters and 120 sentences. Each sentence can have its own recording, or you can save a reading file with Syllables and Reading tools without recordings. To choose the breaks yourself, put each sentence on its own line. An empty line starts a new paragraph.

Show the whole passage on one scrollable page, split automatically into pages, or choose your own page breaks (put --- on its own line, or use Split page in step 3). Start a line with # for a heading.
Right-to-left languages set the file direction automatically. Record non-English passages with your microphone in step 3.

Step 2

The voice (optional)

Use a computer voice here, record your own voice in step 3 with this device's microphone, or skip recordings to make a reading file with Reading tools and Syllables.

The voice reads English. A recording keeps the voice it was made with.

Downloaded one time from Hugging Face, then kept by this browser. On a slow connection this takes a few minutes.

Step 3

Make the recordings (optional)

One recording for each sentence, using your microphone or the computer voice. Listen to each one: a computer voice can say a word wrong. Make it again, or change the sentence in step 1.

Type a passage in step 1 first.

        Saved. What to do next

        1. Send the file to your students the way you send any file: your learning platform, a shared drive or a USB drive. From Google Drive or Google Classroom, a student downloads the file first: the preview there does not run it.
        2. Each student opens it in a browser to read or listen. The file needs no internet and no account.
        3. Keep the file if you want to change it later with Open a file I made. This page does not keep your passage or recordings after you close it.

        If the file does not open on a device (a phone, a tablet, or a school computer that will not open downloaded files), a student opens it on Open a practice file or check: apomera.github.io/AlloFlow/packets-open.html.
