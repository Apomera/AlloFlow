PrintingPress

The Gutenberg-style screw press as a working simulation, plus the materials science, mechanical engineering, economics, history, and typography it touches. Compose your own type, ink the forme, pull the bar, see the impression. Built for interdisciplinary middle-school work. Sources: Gutenberg Museum (Mainz), British L
