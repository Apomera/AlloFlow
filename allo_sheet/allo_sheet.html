    Use 1–40 unique column names and create 1–200 rows. You can rename the neutral defaults before creating the sheet.

    Need .xlsx or full Grist? Open AlloFlow Desktop, or use a district-hosted AlloSheet service configured by your administrator.

    Choose a CSV up to 2 MB, 200 data rows, 40 columns, and 1,200 characters per cell. Files over a limit are rejected without changing the open data.

    Save or reopen a local AlloSheet workspace

    Use one file to keep every table in the bounded local workspace. Reopening validates the file and shows a table-by-table review before replacing anything.

    Protect the downloaded file: AlloSheet workspace JSON is unencrypted and may contain education records. Store and share it only through an approved secure location.

    Workspace files stay on this device unless you choose where to save or upload them. Local analysis results are recalculated rather than stored.
