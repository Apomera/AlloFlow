EvoLab: Evolution

Evolution and natural selection through 17 student modules: seven core simulators, six quick concept labs, three reasoning challenges, and a persistent Capstone investigation. Includes population genetics, common ancestry, climate pressure, coevolution, and Maine wildlife examples.
