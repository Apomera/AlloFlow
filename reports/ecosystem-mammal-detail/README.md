# Mammal visual refinement

Foxes now have folded, ridged ears, a tapered muzzle and smaller nose, eye highlights, and shaped upper/lower legs instead of straight cylinder legs. Rabbits and voles have small muzzle cheeks and eye highlights. All three groups have subtle segmented whiskers attached to their articulated heads.

The new details use the existing geometry/material disposal lifecycle. Whiskers are not separate selection targets; their head attachment preserves forage animation. Existing movement paths, biomass, representative counts, and mushroom spacing are unchanged. Animals remain illustrative teaching models.
