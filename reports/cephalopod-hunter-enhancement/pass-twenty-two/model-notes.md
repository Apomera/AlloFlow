# Coconut half-shell shelter candidate

The candidate replaces only `makeCoconutMesh` and inserts `createCLHuntCoconutGeometry(T)` immediately before it. It converts the two enclosing hemispheres, filled pale sphere and six torus fibers into one hollow half-shell: a rounded brown cortex, an attached irregular ivory cut edge and a visibly concave inner bowl. Fibrous exterior contours and fixed vertex tones provide surface detail without new textures or shaders.

## Reference and interpretation

[Finn, Tregenza and Norman (2009), Defensive tool use in a coconut-carrying octopus](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0960982209019149) reports octopuses carrying coconut shell halves and assembling them as shelter when needed. This is the primary behavioral reference already named by the simulator. [Kew's Cocos nucifera morphology](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A666160-1/general-information) describes the fibrous husk, hard woody shell and internal cavity/endosperm. These support a shell with a distinct outer cortex and open interior rather than a filled ball.

The object is an illustrated, worn half-shell. Cortex thickness, ivory residue and retained coarse fibers are exaggerated to make the object readable at gameplay scale. Its material layers are visual cues rather than a botanical cross-section at measured proportions. The cavity contains no modeled liquid, edible filling or living animal. Loose hair strands, splinter particles and photorealistic microtexture are omitted. The fixed upward-facing bowl makes the hollow legible during transport and at rest; the simulator's existing carrying camouflage bonus remains a gameplay abstraction, not a new claim about immediate protection in the field observations.

## Actual terrain anchor and protected behavior

The source audit established that `updateGround` positions every non-carried shelter root at `terrainHeight + 0.12`. The initial spawn `y = 0.32` is not its final terrain-relative pose. Therefore the authored basal pole is at local `y = -0.12`, while the cut rim rises to roughly `y = +0.23` and the cavity floor remains near `y = -0.051`. This aligns the geometric base with the existing terrain anchor without changing the root frame through the coconut constructor. The silhouette stays inside the original horizontal footprint and vertical envelope. Native captures lack contact shadows, so exact visual floor contact is not inferred from the images alone.

The coconut visual edit is confined to its constructor and adjacent helper. `SHELTER_TYPES`, spawning, distribution, metadata, terrain logic, pickup range, den radius, carrying, dropping, lifetime, announcements and achievements are protected by its exact source-region guard. One wobble RNG draw per spawn remains unchanged. The Group remains unscaled and unrotated, with the same inherited carry placement `(0, -0.1, 0.6)`, rotation wobble and zero drop rotation. The coconut retains its 0.30 camouflage bonus, 0.10 carrying penalty and 90,000 ms placed lifetime. Separately, root integrated the pass's guarded immediate-drop placement fix: the accepted G-drop writes `terrainHeight + 0.12` immediately, so placed shelter defense applies in that same frame. This is a root-owned one-line gameplay correction, not a side effect of the geometry helper.

## Static resource and geometry contract

- One direct mesh, `cl-coconut-shell`, and one independently owned opaque `MeshStandardMaterial`, `cl-coconut-material`. White material color, vertex colors enabled, roughness 0.88 and default front-face rendering. No material callback, shader, uniform, texture, light or per-frame attribute update.
- Root's strict candidate guard confirmed 1,346 vertices, 2,688 triangles and 64,584 bytes of position/normal/color/index data. Guard limits are 1,700 vertices and 3,000 triangles.
- Twenty-eight profile rings with 48 columns plus exterior and interior poles form one closed indexed surface. The opening is a real concavity with a physical rear floor; there is no disk or sphere across the mouth.
- The semantic parts are `outer-cortex`, `ivory-cut-edge` and `inner-bowl`. Index spans are contiguous. Their triangles share the actual rim ring indices; each vertex span describes newly allocated vertices and may have joining triangles that reference the preceding part.
- `geometry.userData.clCoconutGeometry` supplies actual section rings, the outer and inner rim rings, cut-edge crest ring, inner bowl rings, exterior base and interior floor vertex indices, aperture center/axis and cavity ray locators. `clCoconutParts` supplies vertex and index/triangle spans. The center/axis locates the bowl; tests should resolve actual indexed triangles and rings rather than trust metadata as evidence of a cavity.
- Irregular radius, low integrated fiber ribs and smooth angular height variation give the cut rim attached chips and uneven contours. The basal pole stays at the exact ground contact height. Every actor receives independent geometry, buffers and material, so unchanged disposal traversals do not affect neighboring shelters.
