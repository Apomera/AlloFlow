# Geometry World visual enhancement

Geometry World now has a richer architectural presentation, with tactile building materials, a more varied landscape, and a calmer Showcase gallery.

- **Clay and timber detail.** Individually varied brick faces have fine warm mortar and small surface highlights. Timber has broad growth bands, finer fibres, and a restrained knot pattern. Stone keeps its cool palette with finer mineral detail. Small deterministic variations keep adjacent blocks from looking identical.
- **Light across surfaces.** Shared roughness maps give stone, timber, brick, and sand more natural light response in Balanced and Detail. Saver uses the richer painted textures with normal and roughness maps disabled. Undo and Redo now also refresh nearby corner shading after removing a block.
- **Layered scenery.** Lower foothills, staggered mountain ridges, varied conifer groves, and sparse rocks and grasses give Meadow more depth. The scenery remains at least 31.845 units from the lesson ground boundary. Six merged decorative meshes use 5,468 triangles in Detail and 3,292 in Saver. Quality changes rebuild the appropriate landscape and release the old resources.
- **A more deliberate Studio.** The seamless ivory stage has a gentle pool of light, a higher key light, and subtle contact shading based on the actual supporting footprint. Gallery typography, creation metadata, orbit arrows, and pine-and-ivory controls complete the presentation. The final previews include the texture-filtering correction that removes a visible contact-plane edge and the caption contrast refinements found during visual review.
