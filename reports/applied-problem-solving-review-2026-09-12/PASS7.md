# Pass 7: make the final review actionable

The final review now puts **One place to continue** before the checklist. It points to a specific incomplete field, with other prompts collapsed below it. These prompts do not block saving, printing, or continuing with the task.

The checks cover a missing working question or response, a linked artifact without its explanation, evidence rows without a claim or written notes, saved checks without an observation or reasoned decision, missing transfer notes, changed criteria, and a self-rating of “met” without a supporting note. They accept an honest explanation that a test has not happened. Declined AI challenges, unused blank organizers, and optional unrated criteria do not create extra completion requirements.

A bare URL or a reference generated by the source finder no longer counts as written evidence notes. Adding a learner explanation updates the review and evidence-row progress. This is a conservative check of recorded fields: it does not evaluate the quality or accuracy of the reasoning, and unfamiliar citation formats may still count as writing.

**Work on this next**, the checklist cards, and individual evidence/check/criterion edit buttons open the relevant field. Collapsed organizers expand as needed. Linked-work shortcuts land on the explanation or missing link. **Return to my review** brings learners back to the updated checklist. The return context resets when the resource, profile, or mode changes.

No new saved schema or AI request was needed. Learner text, citations, decisions, and source status remain in the existing workspace and export paths. A reviewed set of fields is not an automatic grade, verification, or submission.
