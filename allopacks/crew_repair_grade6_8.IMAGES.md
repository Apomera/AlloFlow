# Crew Launch Week 2: Repair When Something Goes Wrong - image shot list (text-free policy)

Companion to `crew_repair_grade6_8.allopack.json`. The pack ships text-only and renders fully without images. Illustrations, when generated, go into a separate `allopacks/illustrated/` edition with WebP assets under `allopacks/media/crew_repair_grade6_8/`.

Policy: **no raster text, labels, numbers, captions or watermarks in the artwork.** Speech bubbles stay empty or carry a simple icon. Labels and captions live in AlloFlow's native fields. Alt text describes the final artwork and is reviewed against it before shipping. Style: warm, simple, flat; a diverse middle-school Crew of about ten students and one adult; no logos, no school names, no faces of real people.

## Lesson panels (900 px wide; each carries native labels and a caption)

1. `cr-img-punish-vs-repair` - *Two paths.* A split panel: left, a student walking away from a cracked speaker with a padlock icon over a calendar; right, the same student taping the speaker while a Crew member watches. Labels: Punishment, Repair. Caption: one path leaves a cracked speaker; the other gives the Crew its speaker back.
2. `cr-img-fourquestions` - *The four questions.* A Crew leader seated with two students, four plain speech bubbles above with icons: a question mark, a group of dots, a heart, a wrench. Labels: What happened, Who was affected, What do they need, What will you do. Caption: repair puts responsibility to work.
3. `cr-img-cart` - *The quiet version.* A laptop cart with every slot filled and a student closing the door; the room otherwise empty and tidy. Labels: Every slot filled. Caption: stewardship is the habit that means nothing needs repairing.
