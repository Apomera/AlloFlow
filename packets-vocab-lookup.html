  • This page, unlike the vocabulary maker, can reach one other website: the Free Dictionary API, api.dictionaryapi.dev. Its meanings come from Wiktionary. The rule below names that one address, and the browser refuses everything else, this site included.
  • Nothing is sent when the page opens, and nothing is sent while Allow this page to look up words online is off. A lookup starts only when you press Look it up. The switch is not kept: it is off each time the page opens.
  • A lookup sends only the word you typed. Nothing about your students, your other words or your files is sent. No cookies are sent, and no account is used.
  • Like any web request, it carries your IP address and your browser type to that service. The service keeps its own records, under its own privacy policy.
  • The meaning you choose is kept in this browser, with your other changes to the vocabulary questions, on this device only. The vocabulary maker marks it "from the internet, not checked". Read it before you use it: it was not checked for students.
  • This page runs without AI. It looks up, lists and copies, and nothing else.
  • This page loads its own files from this site: the page, three stylesheets, two scripts (packets-page.js, packets-vocab-lookup.js) and the icon in the browser tab.

The rule this page gives your browser. The browser refuses anything the rule does not allow: It is the Content-Security-Policy line near the top of this page's source.

Look up a word online

For a word that is not in the vocabulary word list. The meaning you choose goes into the vocabulary maker, where you can change it.

This browser is not keeping your changes. A meaning you choose here cannot reach the vocabulary maker. This can happen in a private window, or when site data is blocked.

The word

One word or a short phrase, as it is in your list.

When you press Look it up, the word you typed is sent to the Free Dictionary API (api.dictionaryapi.dev), a free service whose meanings come from Wiktionary. Nothing else is sent: not your other words, not your students, not your files. Like any web request, it also carries your IP address.
