Balance emails

One private message per managed student address. Emails show only the total, timestamp, and store availability—not behavior reasons or rankings.

Guardian connections

Use only a verified guardian address and document school authorization. Guardian digests contain positive summary totals and no award reasons, rankings, model files, or student login details. Disabling a connection requires a reason but does not require reaffirming withdrawn consent.

Guardian digest delivery

Send one privacy-minimized progress and balance summary per active, consented guardian connection. Google mail quota and duplicate-period protection still apply.
