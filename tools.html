What happens when you launch?

AlloFlow has several access paths. The finder gives you the relevant preparation steps before sending you into the full application.

Recommended for most educators

Gemini Canvas

Use the shared Canvas experience and Google AI access available to your account. Source-based tools will tell you to add and analyze content first.

Open the stable launcher
Independent and local-first

AlloFlow Desktop

Use the Desktop runtime with supported local or configured providers and local-first storage options.

View Desktop setup
Hosted alternative

Hosted / BYOK

Use the hosted app with a supported AI provider you configure. Tools that do not need AI can still be useful without a provider call.
