Packing Expert

Guide: Packing Expert at world coordinates (5, 1, 7). Use this sheet alongside the lesson world. Observe the named models or guide before answering; sketch and label the model so your reasoning can be understood on paper.

Mentor teaching

Look through the open front of the shipping container. Its usable interior is 6 units long, 2 high, and 3 deep. Each small cube-shaped box is 2 by 2 by 2. Keep the boxes aligned with the container edges. Three fit along the length, one fits in the height, and one fits in the depth: 3 times 1 times 1 gives three boxes. The narrow space at the front remains empty. Volume alone cannot tell us whether a whole box fits.

Question 1 · Step 1

What is the container interior volume? (6 by 2 by 3)

  1. 48 cubic units
  2. 36 cubic units
  3. 24 cubic units

Authored answer: B. 36 cubic units

Question 2 · Step 2

With 2 by 2 by 2 boxes aligned to the container edges, how many fit?

  1. 4 boxes because 36 divided by 8 is more than 4
  2. 6 boxes because the length is 6
  3. 3 boxes: 3 along the length, 1 high, 1 deep

Authored answer: C. 3 boxes: 3 along the length, 1 high, 1 deep

Question 3 · Step 3

Three boxes occupy 3 times 8 = 24 cubic units. How much interior space remains?

  1. 12 cubic units
  2. 4 cubic units
  3. No empty space

Authored answer: A. 12 cubic units

Question 4 · Step 4

Why can a fourth whole box not fit into the remaining front strip?

  1. A container can never hold more than three boxes
  2. The strip is only 1 unit deep, but a box needs 2
  3. The fourth box would have no volume

Authored answer: B. The strip is only 1 unit deep, but a box needs 2

Design Challenge

Guide: Design Challenge at world coordinates (1, 1, 16). Use this sheet alongside the lesson world. Observe the named models or guide before answering; sketch and label the model so your reasoning can be understood on paper.

Mentor teaching

Use the glass platform to build a solid package model with exactly 36 unit cubes. Try 6 by 3 by 2, then a different arrangement such as 4 by 3 by 3. Measure your own blocks and explain how the dimensions multiply to 36. The protected platform is a work surface, not part of your package.

Question 7 · Step 1

Which equals 36? (check each)

  1. 3×3×3 = 27
  2. 6×3×2 = 36 ✓
  3. 5×4×2 = 40

Authored answer: B. 6×3×2 = 36 ✓

Question 8 · Step 2

Which is another way to make 36?

  1. 6×6×1 = 36
  2. Both!
  3. 4×3×3 = 36

Authored answer: B. Both!
