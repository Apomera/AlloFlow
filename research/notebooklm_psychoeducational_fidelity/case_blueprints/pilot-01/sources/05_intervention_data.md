# Reading Intervention and Progress Data

This is a completely synthetic research case. It does not describe a real student.

## Intervention

Jordan participated in a 12-week supplemental reading intervention from December 1, 2025, through March 6, 2026. A licensed reading specialist provided four 30-minute sessions per week in a group of three students. Lessons included explicit phoneme-grapheme mapping, decoding of single- and multisyllabic words, repeated oral reading, and immediate corrective feedback.

Jordan attended 46 of 48 planned intervention sessions. An intervention coach completed four fidelity observations using the program checklist. Mean implementation fidelity was 93%.

## Progress monitoring

The progress measure was a fictional one-minute grade-level oral-reading probe. Scores are the number of words read correctly in one minute. Alternate forms were used. The intervention team's goal was 58 correct words per minute by Week 12.

Jordan's score increased from 31 to 47 correct words per minute. The Week 12 score was 11 words below the goal of 58. The intervention team documented meaningful improvement but insufficient progress to meet the goal and recommended continued intensive reading instruction.
