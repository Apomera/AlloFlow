# Humboldt squid funnel refinement

Root completion: integration and validation are complete. See README.md and validation-summary.json for final 164 distinct unit cases,19 native cases,nine accepted natural views and measured costs. Original author proposals below are retained as historical planning records.

Author status: candidate authored; validation and integration pending. Root alone prepares, checks and integrates runtime candidates. No runtime or mirror changes, test execution, browser/GPU work, staging or commit were performed by the model author. The user's no-commit instruction remains in force.

The accepted mantle, eye surfaces, fins, arm crown, 208 cupped suckers and flattened feeding clubs already give Humboldt squid a clear silhouette. Pass-nine profile and arm close-ups still show a separate straight pipe beneath the head. This candidate replaces that Humboldt-only siphon subtree with one continuous muscular funnel: a broad attached base, a narrower ventral nozzle, a thick rolled outlet and a genuinely open deep interior. The other eleven species retain their four original siphon meshes and materials.

The [Hopkins Marine Station / NOAA external anatomy sheet](https://gillylab.stanford.edu/sites/g/files/sbiybj20896/files/media/file/squids4kidsdgigasanatomy_0.pdf) identifies the funnel on the ventral surface of Humboldt squid. Its accompanying [dissection guide](https://gillylab.stanford.edu/sites/g/files/sbiybj20896/files/media/file/squids4kidsdissectionguide_0.pdf) explains that the muscular funnel ejects mantle water and can change angle, and locates the beak at the center of the arms. These were read as primary anatomical references. The candidate models a fixed illustrative funnel; it does not simulate steering, valves, flow, mantle-cavity internals or a beak. No reference imagery is bundled.

The geometry is authored in the existing `cl-siphon` Group frame. Its position `(0, -.215 * scale, .31 * scale)`, scalar scale and forward `+Z` outlet stay intact. The replacement mesh retains `cl-siphon-tube`; its static coordinates use an identity child transform. The old outer/inner cylinder, torus and flat lumen are replaced by a single opaque `MeshStandardMaterial` draw using the existing `siphonMat` and unchanged tint callback. Linear vertex colors distinguish the pale rolled edge and dark inner recess. The Humboldt branch does not allocate unused old inner/lumen materials. No new textures, shaders, uniforms, random draws or per-frame buffer updates are introduced.

Authored topology is 24 circumferential columns, 24 section rings and two poles: **578 vertices / 1,152 triangles / 27,720 attribute-and-index bytes**. This is an authored count pending root execution. The ceiling is 700 vertices and 1,400 triangles. Three contiguous semantic triangle ranges cover `outer-muscle`, `rolled-outlet` and `inner-recess`. Outer ring 11 and inner ring 15 are physically shared boundary indices; ring 14 is the outlet crest. The inner recess ends in a concave floor around local `(0, .022, -.084)`, approximately .21 behind the forward outlet. This is an actual hollow exterior wall with a recessed interior, not a dark disk across the opening. Each animal owns one geometry and one material for the replacement; Humboldt mesh count intentionally falls from 28 to 25. Geometry grows from the old four primitives' approximately 274 vertices / 380 triangles; no physical-device performance claim is made.

The guarded author script requires the exact original construction string, reverses its single replacement back to identical source bytes, parses the candidate, and checks real indexed geometry, closed edges, visible normals, costs and five actual cavity rays. `--check` writes nothing; `--candidate` writes a separate candidate. The local helper lives inside `createCLHuntAnimal`, preserving existing factory extraction boundaries. Disjoint environment and interaction changes are tolerated because no whole-file baseline equality is needed to apply the exact construction replacement.

The seven new focused cases are designed to exercise actual funnel closure, shared lip seams, unit normals and winding, front-facing cavity and thick wall rays, basal attachment to the actual head/collar triangles, unchanged orientation and color response, independent static resources, no RNG, and comparison of all protected rigs against fingerprints measured from baseline SHA-256 `b34c982127743a9d1f47ed392d0d48d09fa6adba588560bb178ab5e180e5d369`. The permanent compact JSON fixture allows those checks to run in CI without the ignored full baseline file. Independent read-only test review found no mismatch in the physical oracles; execution is still pending.

`prepare-siphon-compatibility.cjs --measure` requires the exact saved b34 source. It measures the new protected-record helper and six existing original fingerprint cases with the narrow Humboldt-only `cl-siphon` ancestor filter applied in memory, asserting that every other expected species hash stays unchanged. It writes the compact permanent fixture and measured local metadata, without editing existing tests. `--check` and `--apply` require that metadata, exact original test SHA values, unchanged oracle/fixture SHA values and reverse byte proofs. Only the six approved ancestor filters and measured Humboldt literals can be migrated. No unrelated geometry, material, shader or species fingerprint may be rebased.

Visual acceptance remains pending matched root captures. Expected limits are a modest silhouette improvement concentrated under the head, an illustrative fixed nozzle rather than a fully deforming funnel, opaque surface shading, and detail that may be subtle at normal dive/phone distance. The accepted head/mantle overlap remains unchanged; adding a recessed beak would require opening the current closed head forecap and is deferred.
