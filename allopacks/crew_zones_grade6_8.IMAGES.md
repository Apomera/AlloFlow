# Crew Launch Week 3: Where Am I Right Now? - image shot list (text-free policy)

Companion to `crew_zones_grade6_8.allopack.json`. The pack ships text-only and renders fully without images. Illustrations, when generated, go into a separate `allopacks/illustrated/` edition with WebP assets under `allopacks/media/crew_zones_grade6_8/`.

Policy: **no raster text, labels, numbers, captions or watermarks in the artwork.** Speech bubbles stay empty or carry a simple icon. Labels and captions live in AlloFlow's native fields. Alt text describes the final artwork and is reviewed against it before shipping. Style: warm, simple, flat; a diverse middle-school Crew of about ten students and one adult; no logos, no school names, no faces of real people.

## Lesson panels (900 px wide; each carries native labels and a caption)

1. `cz-img-priya` - *The one-word check-in.* A student in a Crew circle with a tight jaw and bouncing leg; a small yellow circle floats near her. The Crew leader is looking her way without alarm. Labels: Yellow, The leader notices. Caption: one word tells her what is true and tells the leader she may need a minute.
2. `cz-img-window` - *The window.* A large window frame; inside it a student reading calmly; above the frame the same student with jagged lines around them; below the frame the same student slumped in fog. Labels: Above, Inside, Below. Caption: the goal is to notice when you have left it and to know one move back.
3. `cz-img-reset` - *The move back.* The same student from the first panel, three slow-breath arcs drawn near her mouth, shoulders lowered, the yellow circle now half green. Labels: Three breaths, Shoulders down. Caption: naming a state is not an excuse; it is the first move.
