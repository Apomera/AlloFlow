# Behavior Lens recording follow-up

The daily recording action now opens the observation form directly. Saving a target with **Save target and record** opens that form with the target selected and its observable definition visible.

## Behavior changes

- New observations keep a draft in the existing student-scoped workspace. Today and the ABC workspace offer **Resume observation** when a draft exists.
- Closing or pressing Escape preserves the draft. Discard requires a separate confirmation; saving a new record clears its draft.
- Draft recovery preserves the occurrence time, narrative, optional fields, custom category text, and unfinished AI quick-fill description. Drafts are not counted as saved observations.
- An existing draft takes priority over a newly requested target. A notice explains that its notes and target were preserved.
- Editing a narrative retains an explicitly selected target. Selecting another target does not overwrite an existing narrative.
- Editing an already saved record leaves a separate new-observation draft intact.
- The form leads with plain-language Before / Behavior / After prompts. Preset examples and setting/observer metadata are collapsed, so optional content does not bury the main fields on phones.
- AI quick-fill is collapsed under an optional assistance control. The form footer wraps on phones, and closing a directly opened form returns focus to the recording action.
- Date filters now expose their actual names and selected state. The table action header has a screen-reader label. Muted labels have improved contrast, and the table contains its hidden labels within its horizontal scroll area.
