# Classroom Observation

This is a completely synthetic research case. It does not describe a real student.

Observation date: March 16, 2026  
Setting: Third-grade classroom  
Duration: 30 minutes

The observation included 15 minutes of teacher-led discussion followed by 15 minutes of independent reading and written response.

During the teacher-led discussion, Jordan oriented toward the speaker, raised their hand twice, and gave one response that accurately connected the current lesson to a fact taught the previous day. Jordan followed the transition to independent work without an additional prompt.

During independent reading, momentary time sampling at 1-minute intervals recorded Jordan as academically engaged during 13 of 15 intervals. Jordan used a finger to track text, paused at several unfamiliar words, quietly attempted to sound out four words, and asked the teacher for help with one word. Jordan completed 7 of the 10 assigned sentences during the observation period.

At the end of the lesson, the observer asked five factual questions about the portion Jordan had read independently. Jordan answered 3 of 5 correctly. The teacher then read a comparable short paragraph aloud, and Jordan answered 5 of 5 factual questions correctly. These brief classroom samples are descriptive and are not standardized tests.
