# Geometry World: visual and interaction polish

Geometry World now carries its pine-and-ivory visual style through utility controls, achievement feedback, dimension labels, and exported Showcase images. This pass also improves the continuity between selecting a creation, inspecting it, presenting it, and preparing it for Print Lab.

- **Sharper Showcase images.** Save image renders the scene with a 2048-pixel long edge, preserving aspect and camera framing and respecting device limits. Desktop Meadow produced a 2048 × 1388 PNG; phone Studio produced a 991 × 2048 PNG through the actual postprocessing composer. Saving restores the live renderer and camera state, handles failures, and prevents duplicate saves while encoding.
- **Clearer controls and feedback.** Fly, Undo, Redo, Home, and Clear share consistent SVG icons, 44-pixel minimum targets, and compact history badges. Fly exposes its active state. Achievement cards use a small medal illustration, quieter colors, and space reserved for phone controls; they yield to inspectors, dialogs, Showcase, and short landscape screens.
- **Accurate dimension captions.** Hollow and fractional creations report occupied volume rather than equating their bounding dimensions with solid material. Solid cuboids still show the multiplication formula. Labels use sharper, proportioned canvas textures with readable contrast. Re-measuring releases old labels and cancels pending annotation timers.
- **Reliable building interactions.** Undo/Redo refresh the toolbar immediately. Touch, native pointer lock, view changes, and held flight controls release or retain input appropriately. These transitions no longer leave movement stuck or confuse touch activity with native pointer lock.
- **Selection continuity.** Closing the measurement inspector retains the selected creation, Showcase action, and Print Lab size summary. Connected edits refresh the summary while keeping the inspector closed. Explore measurements explicitly reopens it. Cached summaries validate live block metadata without remeasuring merely because a polling cycle replaced the selection object.
