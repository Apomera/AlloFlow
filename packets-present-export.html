About this page

Where the words, sounds, and pictures come from

Meanings come from pre-built offline word lists (Simple English Wiktionary, English Wiktionary, Open English WordNet, Princeton WordNet, and the New Academic Word List). Sound boxes come from CMUdict mapped to Orton-Gillingham sound labels. Built-in drawings come from Mulberry Symbols and OpenMoji under CC BY-SA 4.0.

What this browser keeps

Your export setup choices stay in local browser storage on this device only.
