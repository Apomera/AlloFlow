Rocks & Minerals

Earth Science: identify 24 rock specimens and 23 minerals by what you can actually see. Interactive landscape cross-section, textured specimen art, a no-AI Visual ID drill, AI Mystery Rock clues, and the real hand-sample tests — Mohs scratch, streak plate, acid fizz — plus a rotatable 3D view of each mineral’s crystal

Open Rocks & Minerals Opens the tool directly — no account, no signup, nothing to install.

Where this fits

Rocks & Minerals is one of the tools in the AlloFlow STEM Lab. It sits in the Earth & Space Science area of the STEM Lab. AlloFlow is free and open source, and it runs in a browser.

Is there a manual for this tool?

Not yet. AlloFlow has written manuals for a few tools so far — see the manuals and guides page — and this one is not among them. The tool itself carries its instructions on screen.
