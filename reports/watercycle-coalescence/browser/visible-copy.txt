Inside-cloud lens: faint cyan points are teaching tracers for invisible water vapor. Rising air cools toward saturation at the glowing cloud base; vapor condenses on tiny nuclei into visible droplets and releases latent heat. In each illustrated collision, two droplets approach, touch, and join into one larger round drop containing their combined water. Further growth may be needed before rain forms. Collision timing is schematic. This transparent cutaway reveals droplets and ice inside the shaded cloud volume. Its level underside marks the modeled saturation base.
