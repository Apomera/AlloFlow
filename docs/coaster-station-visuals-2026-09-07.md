# Station and boarding area refinement

The boarding station now has a pitched two-panel canopy, a translucent ridge, exposed ribs, trim lighting, and contrasting fascia. Theme-aware platform, roof, frame, and floor materials match the surrounding park. Three marked boarding bays, directional floor arrows, paving seams, and a PLATFORM 01 boarding sign make the loading area easier to identify. The main station name uses outward-facing text on both sides rather than mirrored back-face lettering.

Raised platforms now have four columns and concrete pads. Their local transforms are recalculated when the track is rebuilt so pad bottoms remain on the world ground plane and column tops meet the platform underside. Near-ground platforms omit unnecessary foundation columns. These additions are decorative and do not alter track geometry or ride dynamics.

A Station view button in the Build introduction frames the whole station and enters the existing Scene focus mode. Keyboard focus moves to Restore panels and reveals it in the mobile toolbar; it returns learners to editing. This keeps the phone view clear of instruments and editing overlays. The view includes ground-level foundation extents explicitly because the bundled Three r128 Box3 implementation does not include instanced transforms. The camera uses the existing fitted-orbit helper and viewport clearance. Fit coaster restores the whole-track view.

Validation: 270 focused unit tests passed, including foundation height checks and existing coaster/camera contracts. The browser workflow passed after the clear-view refinement and covers theme views, keyboard focus, camera framing, a raised station after import, grounded pad transforms, FX Lite, phone framing, and unchanged analysis across theme/camera changes. Daylight, neon, and elevated phone views were visually reviewed. Main and desktop hashes match; the scoped diff check passed.
