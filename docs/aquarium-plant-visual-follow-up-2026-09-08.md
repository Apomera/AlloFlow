# Aquarium plant visuals and inspection follow-up — 8 September 2026

This bounded follow-up covers the active Aquarium plant catalog, its scene bridge, the 3D plant meshes, and selected-plant inspection. The catalog contains **14 unique IDs across 19 tank entries**. Vallisneria and eelgrass are not active entries. This is a growth-form specification, not a claim that every trade name resolves to a verified species or physical specimen.

Before this pass, the renderer in `stem_lab/stem_tool_aquarium.js`, `addPlant` (around lines 11983–12017), classified joined name/profile text using regular expressions. This collapsed Java fern into pinnate fronds, Anubias into a basal rosette, Monte Carlo into grass blades, Java moss into spheres, and several fine-leaved stems into broad blades. Mangrove foliage remained submerged. Chaetomorpha already had a useful strand treatment, but its metadata called it moss. These distinctions are instructional content: the learner should see why a creeping rhizome, basal crown, floating frond, and algal filament are different structures.

The integrated `getAquariumPlantVisualProfile(id)` contract returns `id`, `label`, `growthForm`, recommended `placement`, `identification`, `variation`, `scaleNote`, and `modelNote`. An own-property guard rejects unknown/prototype IDs. The bridge carries this descriptor alongside actual `zone`, `biomass`, `maxBiomass`, `health`, and `photosynthesisActive`. Recommended placement must not silently overwrite a learner's assigned zone. The inspector should present the identifying feature and scope beside the selected specimen; selection and camera close-up must not mutate biology, clock, or placement. Keep the native selector before the canvas, a visible return-to-whole-tank action, and a direct route from selected plant to its size control. Close-up needs keyboard and illustrated-fallback equivalents.

Preserve explicit zero biomass: no live foliage, with accessible selection and the biomass reading still available. Mesh scale follows a relative biomass index, not centimeters or grams. Health-driven browning is an illustrative model cue, not a diagnosis of a measured leaf symptom. Do not generate nutrient-specific lesions, color-based fertilizer advice, or oxygen bubbles as if photosynthesis necessarily produces visible pearling.

A material model limitation belongs near floating/emergent inspection. Mangrove and floating leaves access air, yet the present simulation uses shared plant gas-flow coefficients for dissolved-water contributions. The morphology pass leaves those equations unchanged and discloses this limitation. Floating plants require space at and above the surface; the display must preserve that interface. [UF/IFAS growth-form overview](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/FA251).

One related catalog correction is justified now: the brackish mangrove fact says roots excrete salt crystals. Red mangroves primarily exclude salt at roots, while secretion through leaf glands occurs in other mangrove groups. Replace the claim with that distinction. [NOAA mangrove guide](https://response.restoration.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/Oil_Spill_Mangrove.pdf). Other care-profile claims remain outside this bounded visual review; the existing wording about rapid nitrate removal, iron producing red Rotala, and trade-name origins should not acquire new visual authority simply because meshes improve.
