# Referral and Educational Records

This is a completely synthetic research case. It does not describe a real student.

## Student and referral

Jordan Lee is an 8-year, 10-month-old third-grade student. Jordan's classroom teacher and family requested an evaluation because Jordan continues to have difficulty reading unfamiliar words accurately and reading connected text fluently despite targeted intervention. The evaluation questions concern Jordan's academic pattern, response to reading intervention, and instructional needs.

## Educational history

Jordan has attended the same public elementary school continuously since kindergarten. School records document access to the school's core reading and mathematics curricula across kindergarten through third grade. Jordan's attendance was 95% in first grade, 96% in second grade, and 96% during the current school year through March 20, 2026.

English is the language used by Jordan at home and at school. Records do not identify a history of English-learner services. School hearing and vision screenings completed on September 18, 2025, were recorded as passed. Records do not identify a motor disability or an educationally relevant medical condition.

## School performance

Second-grade report cards described mathematics as a consistent strength and reading accuracy and fluency as persistent concerns. During third grade, Jordan has met classroom expectations in mathematics, science discussions, and oral presentations. Independent reading assignments have required additional time and teacher support.

The third-grade team documented that Jordan received evidence-based core reading instruction from a licensed classroom teacher. The team referred Jordan for supplemental reading intervention after fall screening and continued the intervention because progress-monitoring scores improved but remained below the intervention goal.
