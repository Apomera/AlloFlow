**Not integrated or deployed.** Changes are confined to `.tmp/studio-audio18/` and this report package. Shared source, host, reader, generated modules, catalogs and Git were not written. No installation, app server, provider call, microphone use, real-user saved-state operation or communication with other sessions occurred. No applicable AGENTS.md was found in the inspected ancestry or target paths.
