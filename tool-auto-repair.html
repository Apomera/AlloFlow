Auto Repair Shop

Diagnose, repair, and maintain a vehicle. OBD-II code reading, listening / fluid / visual diagnosis, 7 step-by-step repair scenarios (oil change, brakes, alternator, tires, air filter, A/C, timing belt), tool selection mini-game, safety modules (jack stands, electrical, refrigerant). Career data for ASE certification +

Open Auto Repair Shop Opens the tool directly — no account, no signup, nothing to install.
