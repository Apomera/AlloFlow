Before you share

Describe the task and the result, but use examples that do not identify a student or disclose protected material.

Public GitHub issues can be read by anyone. Use the private email route when a classroom story needs more context, and remove identifying details first.

Protect people and systems

Do not include sensitive material

Whether you use GitHub or email, do not send:

  • student names, records, work samples with identifiers, or other education records;
  • health, disability, assessment, or clinical information about an identifiable person;
  • API keys, passwords, access tokens, credentials, or private security details;
  • confidential school or organization information; or
  • copyrighted source text, paid content, or files you do not have permission to share.

For a potential security vulnerability, use private email and provide only the minimum safe detail needed to begin a conversation. Never paste a working credential.

Useful, not lengthy

What helps us understand

A few concrete details are more useful than a polished review.

  1. Name the tool or workflow.Say what you opened and, if relevant, whether you used Gemini Canvas, the browser app, Desktop, a Skill, or the MCP connector.
  2. Describe the goal.Explain what you were trying to accomplish without sharing the original private source or identifying a learner.
  3. Explain what happened.Include the steps you took, the result you expected, and the result you saw. A de-identified screenshot can help when it is safe to share.
  4. Add access context when relevant.Name the browser, device, assistive technology, input method, or file type involved.

What happens next

Feedback is reviewed, but a response is not guaranteed

AlloFlow is an open-source project, not a staffed support desk. Public issues make ideas and progress visible to the community, but there is no guaranteed response time, fix date, or commitment to implement a request. Private messages may receive a reply when follow-up would be useful.

This channel is not appropriate for emergencies, legally required reporting, or urgent student support. Follow your school or organization’s established process for those needs.
