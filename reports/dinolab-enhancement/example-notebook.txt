Microraptor (Microraptor gui)
Early Cretaceous | 125–120 mya | Asia (China)
Catalog evidence: Many beautifully preserved specimens from China’s Jehol deposits.
Catalog uncertainty: Exactly how it used four wings is still modeled.

My question:
How do four wings change gliding?

Evidence I noticed:
Feathers are preserved on both arms and legs.

My explanation:
The leg feathers may have helped control a glide.

What I still wonder:
The fossil alone cannot show the exact flight posture.
