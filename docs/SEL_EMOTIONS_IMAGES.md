# Emotion illustrations for the Emotion Explorer (SEL Hub)

The tool draws every family and feeling as an emoji. This is the shot list for replacing them with illustrations, one per feeling, so a student who does not read emoji faces the way the author did (or reads them differently across cultures) gets a fuller picture. Generated with Gemini or ChatGPT, or drawn, or licensed stock; the policy is the same.

**Policy:** no text, letters, numbers or captions in the artwork. One child, chest-up, expressing the feeling with face and posture, against a plain soft background; a different child for each image and a mix of skin tones, hair, glasses, hijab, hearing aids, wheelchairs, ages 10 to 14. Square, 256 px WebP, under 20 KB each. No real people. Nothing frightening for the hard feelings: sad is sad, not despairing; scared is wide-eyed, not in danger. The word is always printed beside the image, so the image carries the expression, not the label.

**Wiring:** put files in `sel_hub/media/emotions/` and list them in `manifest.json` there (`"feelings": { "proud": "proud.webp" }`, `"families": { "happy": "family-happy.webp" }`). Anything not listed keeps its emoji. Mirror the folder to `desktop/web-app/public/sel_hub/media/emotions/`.
