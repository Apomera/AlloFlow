## Finding

The pass-thirteen `reef-rock-detail.png` shows the accepted irregular rock finish next to smooth, saturated coral fingers. Their silhouettes are distinct and their rounded tips are intact, but the largely uniform pink surfaces read as molded plastic. The antler and corymbose forms use the same finish, so a shared surface treatment can improve all three without rebuilding their silhouettes. The common-octopus inspection image confirms that the surrounding scene now has enough surface detail for the coral's simplicity to stand out.

In `stem_lab/stem_tool_cephalopodlab.js`, `createReefCoralGeometry(height,index)` already constructs three curved forms, transported branch frames, rounded caps and vertex colors. `grow()` gives branches a colony-height shade from approximately 0.66 to 0.94, with only a small tip adjustment. The material is a single StandardMaterial with roughness 0.84 and an sRGB-decoded tint blended 16% toward a neutral color. Its `reefSurface` hook adds only a product of sine waves to albedo; it has no pore relief and no screen-footprint filtering.

One important boundary: `reefSurface` is still the **rock fallback when derivatives are unavailable**. Replacing that function globally would change a protected rock path. A new coral-only hook is the appropriate boundary.

## Approved single upgrade

Add branch-following, softly recessed surface pits while retaining the existing growth gradient exactly. These are illustrative corallite-like details, not a taxonomically calibrated polyp model. The established pink, orange, gold and mauve categories remain, without white tips, emissive color or an implied bleaching simulation. Root explicitly narrowed the proposal to preserve all original color values as well as the geometry.

Inside the existing `grow()` loop, the patch adds one static Float32 vec4 attribute, `clCoralSurface`: circumferential cell coordinate, accumulated centerline distance divided by 0.055 plus a deterministic branch/colony offset, detail fade, and integer circumferential cell count. The count is 6–29. All original positions, normals, colors and indices remain exact. The attribute follows curved branches without planar projection or a stack of 3D noise. Detail fades at basal joins and before rounded tips; all hemisphere rings have zero detail.

`shadeCLHuntCoralSurface` evaluates one isolated, soft pore per staggered two-dimensional cell. Its center jitters within ±0.13 cells and its radius varies from 0.17 to 0.215 cells, with a soft outer lip of 0.06 cells. The entire pore stays inside its cell, avoiding a neighborhood search. Hash inputs wrap by the integer circumferential cell count so duplicate seam vertices agree. This avoids sine bands and a perfectly uniform dotted grid. It still needs visual review; deterministic jitter alone does not establish natural appearance.

The scalar pore field is bounded to −0.85…0.18. Its albedo multiplier is 0.788…0.9116, effective roughness is clamped to 0.87…0.96, and the tangent-normal slope is capped at 0.08 (under 4.58 degrees). Relief strength is 0.0012 metres per field unit, giving at most 1.02 mm of illustrative depression. Surface derivatives produce the view-space normal adjustment; detail fades between 0.2 and 0.7 cells per pixel. Raw relief is differentiated before applying that weight, avoiding nested derivatives. WebGL2 or `OES_standard_derivatives` enables the hook and static cache key `cl-coral-surface-v14`; unsupported WebGL1 retains the original `reefSurface` finish.

Independent shader review found an additional precision risk: the integer-valued period is smoothly interpolated, so a tiny fractional error could enter the wrapped hash. In the original field, period 13 plus 1e-6 produced a 0.984 field-unit seam discrepancy for one sampled row. The narrow refinement rounds the period and snaps U only within a relative 5e-7 window of its two seam boundaries. This covers four Float32 ULPs for every production period from 6 to 29. In-memory old/candidate comparison passed 17,088 unchanged integer-input samples, zero discrepancy for all tested ±1/±4-ULP seam cases, and a maximum near-seam correction of 0.00013136 field units (under 0.0000158 in the albedo multiplier). No other source changes. The focused unit now exercises actual Float32 neighboring values as well as ideal integer seams.

The remaining code review found no additional defect: triangles stay within a single branch and constant period; branch joins and cap rows fade to zero; raw-field derivatives precede the footprint weight; the normal sign matches r128's front-face bump convention; and color/roughness/normal injections occur in the proper bundled shader order. Filtering uses the larger screen footprint of the two surface coordinates. Temporal shimmer still requires the planned moving-camera visual check; scalar and static-image checks cannot establish that result.
