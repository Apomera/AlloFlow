Choose a water-saving plan for a school garden

Student task

Challenge brief

In this fictional classroom scenario, a school garden team wants to reduce water waste while keeping its plants healthy. Compare two possible approaches before recommending a plan. The school has not collected local measurements yet.

Which watering plan should the garden team try first, and what evidence would change your recommendation?

A short recommendation with reasons, one tradeoff, and a plan for a small check.

Teacher-verified lesson facts

  • Water can enter the soil through infiltration.
  • Water at the soil surface can evaporate.
  • Water that does not soak into the ground may run off.

What your response needs

  • Use two lesson ideas accurately.
  • Compare at least two possible approaches.
  • Explain a tradeoff and a way to check the recommendation.

Limits and unknowns

  • Use available classroom materials for a small trial.
  • Treat unsupplied costs and measurements as unknowns.
  • How quickly does the garden soil absorb water?
  • What resources and time can the team realistically use?
