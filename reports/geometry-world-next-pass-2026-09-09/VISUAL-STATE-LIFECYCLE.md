# Geometry World visual-state lifecycle audit

The bounded source audit and actual-THREE reproductions found three scene-isolation problems: replacing terrain during Studio exposed the new group; delayed selection layers could appear after Studio entry or a reset; and newly created dust motes bypassed Studio's initial visibility snapshot. These paths are now fixed in the canonical core and desktop mirror. Photo capture also respects the selected postprocessing profile, and Saver hides only authored-ground edge overlays.

## Confirmed defects and changes

| Trigger | Reproduced behavior | Implemented change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change detail to Saver while Studio is active | The old landscape's six geometries were disposed, but its replacement was visible and absent from the Studio restoration list. | Capture intended visibility before replacement; remove the disposed group from the list; register and hide its replacement. Meadow restores the original visibility. |
| Enter Studio while a measured build is still revealing layers | A later layer appeared visibly after the initial annotations were hidden. | Every new glow joins the Showcase hidden list, including delayed layers. |
| Reset or tear down during a selection reveal | Existing glows survived clearWorld, and a pending callback created another glow after the engine was destroyed. | Track reveal/fade timers, cancel them on clearWorld, and guard each callback with a revision and destroyed check. |
| Measure A, wait 24 seconds, then measure B | A's old fade can clear the newer selection soon after it is shown. | Each measurement owns its fade timeout and interval; clearing or replacing it cancels that work. |
| Wait in Studio or Meadow Showcase with ambient effects enabled | A newly spawned mote remained visible because it was created after the one-time hide pass. | Do not spawn ambient motes while Showcase is active. Existing motes retain their ordinary expiry cleanup. |
| Take a Photo in Saver with a composer object present | Photo used the composer despite the live pipeline disabling postprocessing. | Honor `_postFxEnabled !== false`, with the existing direct-render fallback on composer failure. |
| Render dense ground in Saver | Every authored ground block retained an edge-overlay draw call. | Hide only shared block-edge LineSegments under ground parents; restore them when returning to detail. Preserve build edges, parent visibility, geometry, picking and history. |

Disposed selection glows are also removed from both Showcase restoration lists. The previous terrain group is never reattached during restoration; the original defect was unintended replacement visibility and a stale retained reference, rather than duplicate terrain being reinserted.

## Other audited transitions and follow-up priorities

Lesson loading already ends Showcase before clearing the old world, restores the camera/environment, clears retained builder selection, and rebuilds terrain for the next lesson bounds. Dimension-label callbacks already have a revision guard, owned texture disposal and cancellation on clearWorld. The builder's periodic selection outline also explicitly hides itself during Showcase. These mechanisms were retained.

Reduced-motion behavior remains inconsistent and deserves a separate focused pass. The actual profile function selects Saver for Auto plus an OS reduced-motion preference, but an explicit Detailed profile enables ambient motion again. Even with `_ambientMotionEnabled === false`, the current NPC body update moves its Y coordinate from 0.75 to 0.84093 in the reproduction; only the newer eye and arm animation block is gated. Entry swoop, walking head bob and break shake also lack a reduced-motion guard in the inspected code. The preference is sampled at initialization/action time, with no live media-query change listener. These observations are documented without expanding this implementation scope.

Theme styles already include high-contrast rules for the builder dock, launcher and Showcase controls, and reduced-motion CSS disables their transitions. This pass did not perform a fresh browser contrast audit. Floating measurement sprites use a fixed ivory-on-pine treatment and cannot inherit page CSS; any future theme-specific annotation styling should be explicit and checked in the rendered scene.

The horizon plane remains fixed at Y −0.03 while landscape bases follow the lesson ground Y. Elevated authored lesson grounds therefore merit a visual check for a lower surrounding plane before extending the world generator to more vertical terrain. This is a source observation, not a confirmed screenshot regression from this pass.
