OpticsLab AP

Geometric + wave optics: ray diagrams, Snell's law, mirrors, lenses, double-slit interference, single-slit diffraction, polarization in 3D. Side-by-side draggable sims + calculators with show-the-math, sample problems, glossary, misconceptions, exam-style quiz, and AI-graded explanations.

Open OpticsLab AP Opens the tool directly — no account, no signup, nothing to install.
