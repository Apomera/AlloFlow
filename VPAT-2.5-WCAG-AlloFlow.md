## Standards and interpretation

The engineering target is [WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). This report follows the [ITI VPAT and ACR framework](https://www.itic.org/policy/accessibility/vpat); it does not establish legal compliance. Criterion 4.1.1 Parsing was removed in WCAG 2.2 and is not counted among the 55 A/AA criteria below. Level AAA is not evaluated or included.

## Conformance level terms

| Term | Definition used in this report |
|---|---|
| **Supports** | The functionality of the product has at least one method that meets the criterion without known defects or meets with equivalent facilitation. |
| **Partially Supports** | Documented exceptions establish that some relevant product functionality fails the criterion. Missing evidence alone is insufficient. |
| **Does Not Support** | The majority of product functionality does not meet the criterion. |
| **Not Applicable** | The criterion is not relevant to the product. |

**Pending determination** is a working-draft status, not an ITI conformance level. It marks incomplete release-level assessment and is not a failure, a pass, or Not Applicable. ITI reserves **Not Evaluated** for Level AAA. The two prior Not Applicable assumptions(2.5.4 and 1.2.4) require a current applicability inventory. Complete the evidence and template review before issuing a formal ACR; the existing Markdown format and WCAG edition reference are retained. [ITI framework](https://www.itic.org/policy/accessibility/vpat) and [report-writing guidance](https://www.itic.org/documents/accessibility/VPATModule2_PreptoWrite.pdf).
