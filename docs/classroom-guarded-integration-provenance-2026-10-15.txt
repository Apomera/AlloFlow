Selective adaptation
This integration adapts the keyed converter and reviewed membership workflow,
not the entire old teacher component. Private labels remain separate. Current
reader, Guided, Family, materials and onboarding work is preserved. The existing
safe-update planner is retained, with support for a public linked-class marker.

New integration hardening
Versioned/pair-approved MessageChannel sessions; revocation and operation IDs;
atomic roster/key persistence with cooperating-tab conflict detection; exclusions
from ordinary backup and Canvas preferences; explicit key recovery/management;
first-link setup preservation; no silent random-ID downgrade or retired-name reuse.
