# African Storybook mirror rights

Last audited: 2026-07-25

African Storybook states that its stories are openly licensed and may be read,
downloaded, printed, copied, adapted, and translated without requesting
permission or paying a fee, provided the writers, illustrators, translators,
copyright holders, and African Storybook source are acknowledged. Some
individual editions add a noncommercial restriction.

AlloFlow imports only storybooks identified by the official catalog as
ASb-approved. The importer then audits every edition's back cover and preserves
its exact Creative Commons variant, creator roles, copyright holder, source
URL, original-source URL when supplied, reading level, language, page text, and
illustration pairing.

Import rules:

- CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC, and CC BY-NC-SA editions may be mirrored with
  their title-level conditions.
- Noncommercial editions set `usagePolicy.commercial` to `false`.
- NoDerivatives and unrecognized or versionless licenses fail closed.
- Creator attribution is stored both as structured contributor records and as
  a readable attribution string.
- Text is stored locally for accessible reading and transformation.
- Illustrations remain source-hosted at African Storybook and retain their
  original page association; no OCR-derived substitute replaces the artwork.
- The initial collection is limited to approved editions in Somali, Tigrinya,
  Kirundi, Lingala, Lesotho Sesotho, and Wolof.

Sources:

- Approved catalog: https://www.africanstorybook.org/lists/booklist.approved.php
- Terms of use: https://www.africanstorybook.org/terms.html
- Project home: https://www.africanstorybook.org/
- Creative Commons licenses: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/

African Storybook can be contacted at `africanstorybook@saide.org.za` regarding
a future full-catalog feed or coordinated bulk mirroring. The public licenses
do not require permission for this noncommercial accessibility mirror, but
provider coordination is recommended before substantially increasing request
volume.
