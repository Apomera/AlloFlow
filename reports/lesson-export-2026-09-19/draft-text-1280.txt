Compare fractions on a number line
Direct-instruction segment · 15 minutes

1. Model equal intervals · Direct instruction
Minutes: 5
Teacher says: Short
Learners do: Learners place fraction cards on their number lines and compare their models with a partner.
Check for understanding: How do you know where one half belongs?
Possible learner response: It is halfway between zero and one because the spaces are equal.
If learners need support (likely misconception): Fold a paper strip into equal halves together, then match the fold to the number line.
If learners are ready to go further: Compare one half with three fourths and explain your answer using equivalent fractions.

Minutes: 5
