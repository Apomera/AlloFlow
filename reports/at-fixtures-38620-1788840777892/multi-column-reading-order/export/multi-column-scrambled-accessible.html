The Water Cycle (scrambled-stream test: read 1 to 8 in order)

  1. Evaporation: the sun heats water into invisible vapor.
  2. Condensation: rising vapor cools into clouds and fog.
  3. Precipitation: heavy droplets fall as rain or snow.
  4. Collection: water gathers in oceans and the cycle repeats.
  5. Groundwater: water seeps down and is stored underground.
  6. Transpiration: plants release water vapor from leaves.
  7. Runoff: surface water flows downhill into rivers.
  8. Human impact: cities and dams change how water moves.
