Completed locally on 2026-10-04. All changes remain unstaged and uncommitted.

Saved investigation actions now identify the exact original shown on the card. Rename, download, replay, use saved B as A and remove recheck its complete canonical record against the latest current workspace. A stale or moved card refreshes without changing the saved originals or current draft. Duplicate identifiers have a plain explanation and stable card keys; editing a title does not replace its input.

Remove deletes one selected record. **Undo remove** restores the last removal in the current page session near its removal-time position, with current record order kept. Exact original multiplicity prevents an extra restoration while allowing other same-ID originals to remain. Full archives and unavailable originals keep a described blocked Undo control. The archive holds up to 12 records. Undo is not a persistent or multiple-removal history.

Backup merging preserves every current saved original, including duplicate identifiers. Incoming occurrences with IDs absent from the current archive are retained, including duplicate occurrences; the existing policy keeps current originals when an incoming record shares a current ID. Capacity overflow rejects the merge. Prototype-like identifiers are handled safely. Duplicate IDs remain unavailable in the existing paired saved-review selectors.

An actual draft-only backup can fill an untouched workspace. Missing backup workspaces do not replace current work with a manufactured draft. A successful saved import clears the session archive Undo; rejected or failed imports retain it. Stale and blocked archive actions retain the recently removed reading tray; successful notebook replacement follows the existing tray rules.
