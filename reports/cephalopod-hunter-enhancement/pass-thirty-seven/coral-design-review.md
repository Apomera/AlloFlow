# Coral candidate review

This is an unexecuted proposal for the accepted e6adbd73f260f5eec6ba8419cd1b3e5dee126f2ed81c0199ac83d5abf626f257 source. No repository files, tests, browser state, server or GPU state were changed by this review.

The pass36 saved natural desktop view has clear distinct finger, antler and corymbose silhouettes, but its nearest colonies still read as evenly colored rods. The pass14 closeups show that the existing pores already provide useful restrained relief. Keep their shader, derivative filtering, seam behavior and normal slope bound unchanged.

The two unique replacements in guards.json affect only the static color buffer. They add broad tissue tone with amplitude at most 0.028, faded to zero by the existing surfaceFade at the substrate and growth cap. The wave is computed from the deterministic branch/index identifiers, longitudinal coordinate and cos(angle), so both seam vertices receive the same value and no dive RNG is consumed. It is computed once during geometry construction. A smoothstep over the final 34 percent of a branch adds up to 0.035 of brightness and keeps the hemisphere rings smooth. The existing palette hue and RGB weighting remain in place. All channels remain in [0,1].

The hemisphere cap's +0.035 shade lift is intentional and affects color only. This is 3.5 percentage points of the vertex shade multiplier, rather than exactly 3.5 percent of its previous value. The green color component increases by 0.035 before Float32 rounding, and the red/blue components use their existing tip weights. Basal colors are exactly original: t is zero, growthTip is zero and surfaceFade is zero. Every cap ring has zero tissue mottle; its geometry, smooth normals and pore fade remain original.

This is deliberately modest. It is intended to give broad local tissue variation and a slightly clearer growth-tip transition while preserving colony placement and shape. It does not resolve the visible intersections of independently generated branch tubes. Addressing those would require geometry/topology work with substantially larger normal and footprint risk. I recommend preserving that geometry for this pass.

Inspect the near finger/antler/corymbose views alongside the natural desktop and phone views. Reject the proposal if it produces conspicuous bands or bright caps; the intended change is broad and restrained.
