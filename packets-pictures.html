This page runs without AI. No account. It can search three named websites for pictures, and only after you turn that on. See the details Hide the details
  • This page, unlike most Packets pages, can reach other websites. Most of the other pages cannot connect to any other website: the guide for school IT names the two that can, and what they send. This one can connect to the five addresses listed here, and to nothing else: the rule below names them, and the browser refuses everything the rule does not name.
  • Global Symbols: globalsymbols.com. Openverse: api.openverse.org. Wikimedia Commons: commons.wikimedia.org, with its picture files at thumb.wikimedia.org and upload.wikimedia.org.
  • Nothing is sent when the page opens, and nothing is sent while Search for pictures online is off. A search starts only when you press Search.
  • A search sends only the word you search for to those three services. Then the browser asks them for the pictures it shows. Nothing about your students, your other words or your files is sent. No cookies are sent, and no account is used.
  • Like any web request, each one carries your IP address and your browser type to that service. The browser also tells the service which website asked: this site's address, not the page and not what is on it. Each service keeps its own records, under its own privacy policy.
  • A picture from your device is read in this browser and is not uploaded.
  • A picture you choose is made small (256 pixels at most) and kept, with its credit, in this browser's storage for this site (IndexedDB), on this device only. Remove and Remove all delete it.
  • The switch is remembered only if you turn on Keep this on, on this device. That one setting is then kept in this browser's local storage. On a shared computer, leave it off.
  • Only pictures that state a license that allows copying with credit are shown: public domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA. Pictures labeled as not for commercial use or as not to be changed are left out, and so are pictures with no stated license.
  • Pictures and symbol sets labeled as made with AI are left out. A picture that is not labeled that way cannot be told apart.
  • This page runs without AI. It searches, lists and copies, and nothing else.
  • This page loads seven files, all from this site: the page itself, two stylesheets (packets.css, packets-pictures.css), three scripts (packets-page.js, packets_my_words.js, packets-pictures.js) and the icon in the browser tab. It loads no fonts and no trackers. Pictures from the three services are loaded only after a search.
  • There are no cookies and nothing to sign in to.
  • Like any website, the server that sends these files can see that your browser asked for them. It does not see your word: the part of an address after the # sign is never sent.

The rule this page gives your browser. The browser refuses anything the rule does not allow: It is the Content-Security-Policy line near the top of this page's source.
