TIME INVESTIGATION | Could T. rex have hunted Stegosaurus?
Initial prediction: yes
Catalog age estimates: T. rex 68–66 mya; Stegosaurus 155–145 mya
Selected evidence: Their known age ranges do not overlap.
My explanation: I first thought yes. Stegosaurus lived about 155–145 mya, much earlier than T. rex, so they could not meet.
Transfer pair: T. rex and Triceratops (68–66 mya)
My transfer conclusion: Overlap makes an encounter possible in time, but does not prove hunting.
