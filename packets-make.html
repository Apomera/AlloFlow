AlloFlow Packets What a practice file can and cannot do
This page runs without AI. No account. The words you choose are not uploaded. See the details Hide the details
  • Nothing is generated when you make a file or when a student uses it. The spoken sounds were recorded ahead of time with two AI voices (Kokoro and Google Gemini).
  • The practice file is put together in this browser tab. The page looks your words up in a word list and a sound dictionary that it has already loaded. Your words and your choices are not sent anywhere.
  • There are no cookies and nothing to sign in to.
  • The page loads files from this site only. At step 3 it gets the sound files, also from this site. Most are the same files whatever you choose. Some words have their sounds in small files of about 40 words each, and the page gets only the ones your words need.
  • Like any website, the server that sends these files can see that your browser asked for them. That includes which of the small sound files were asked for, but not which words. It receives nothing else.
  • On this device the page keeps your last choices and the sound files. The buttons that remove them are under About this page, at the bottom.
  • What you add to a word (its sounds, your recording, your picture) is kept in this browser's storage for this site. It is not uploaded. It goes only into the files you make. You can remove it under My words on this device, in step 2.
  • The microphone is used only when you press Record it yourself, and only until the recording stops. The recording stays on this device.
  • This page reaches only this site. One other page, Find a picture, can search Global Symbols, Openverse and Wikimedia Commons after you turn its search on there. It sends them the word you search for, and asks them for the pictures it shows.

Every file this page loads: they are listed in the page's source, and all of them are on this site.

The rule this page gives your browser. The browser refuses anything the rule does not allow: It is the Content-Security-Policy line near the top of this page's source.

            Saved. What to do next

            1. Send the file to your students the way you send any file: your learning platform, a shared drive or a USB drive.
            2. Each student opens it in a browser, finishes it and presses the main button at the end, Save results as a spreadsheet file (CSV). They hand that file back to you.
            3. Add the results up on Add up results, or open them in a spreadsheet. In AlloFlow: Class Analytics, Add take-home results.

            On a phone or tablet, a student opens the file on Open a practice file: apomera.github.io/AlloFlow/packets-open.html.

            Fill in a student's code name for them

            When the file is on a website or a shared link, add #learner=CodeName to the end of the link, for example #learner=BlueHeron7. It stays on the student's device.

            Make one for each student

            One file for each student, with the student's code name filled in. They come in one .zip file. Each file is the same as the one above, made with that code name.

            Use code names, not real names. At most 40 at a time.
            More choices The first student gets A, the second B, the third C, then A again. Students who sit together then have different files.

            About this page

            What this page keeps

            Your last choices are kept in this browser so that the page opens the way you left it: the activities, how you chose the words, any words you typed or checked in the list, the version and the length. The sound files are kept too, so that they are not downloaded again. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

            What you add to a word (its sounds, your recording, your picture) is kept in this browser's storage for this site, until you remove it under My words on this device, in step 2. It is not uploaded.

            Where the words and sounds come from

            • The spoken sounds were recorded ahead of time with two AI voices (Kokoro and Google Gemini). A word you record yourself in step 2 uses your recording. Each practice file lists its credits under About this file.
