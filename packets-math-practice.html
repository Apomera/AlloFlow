A practice file can:

  • Show each fact, take the answer on a number pad and say right away whether it is right.
  • Show the answer after a wrong try, ask the student to type it while looking, then ask again.
  • Keep the student's place on that device, and save a results file to that device when the student presses the save button.

A practice file cannot:

  • Run AI. Nothing is generated when you make a file or when a student uses it.
  • Connect to the internet. The file tells the browser to refuse every connection, and the browser enforces it.
  • Keep its answers secret. They are inside the file, so that it can say right or wrong with no internet.
  • Time the student. There is no clock.

      About this page

      What this page keeps

      Your last choices are kept in this browser so that the page opens the way you left it: the kind of facts, the set, your own facts, the way to practice, the facts you chose as known and the title. A student's code name is never kept. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

      Where the sets and the two ways come from

      • The sets and their order follow a common teaching order, not a standard. Addition: + 0 and + 1, doubles, make 10, near doubles, then the rest. Multiplication: × 2, × 10, × 5, × 1 and × 0, squares, × 3, × 4, × 9, × 6, × 7, × 8. Subtraction, the addition facts taken apart: − 0 and − 1, doubles, from 10, near doubles, then the rest within 10 and within 20. Division, the multiplication facts undone: ÷ 2, ÷ 10, ÷ 5, ÷ 1 and 0 ÷, squares, ÷ 3, ÷ 4, ÷ 9, ÷ 6, ÷ 7, ÷ 8.
      • Cover, copy, compare and incremental rehearsal are well-known ways to practice math facts. Both give feedback on every answer. They are for a student who can work a fact out but does not know it by heart yet.
      • Rehearsal with known facts follows one fixed plan. Each new fact gets a run: the new fact and a known fact, the new fact and 2 known facts, then the new fact and 3 known facts. Each new fact comes back once more later, with 2 known facts. So 8 of every 12 cards are facts the student knows. The first time a new fact comes, it is shown with its answer.
      • Every answer is worked out in this tab by plain arithmetic. The same choices always make the same file.
