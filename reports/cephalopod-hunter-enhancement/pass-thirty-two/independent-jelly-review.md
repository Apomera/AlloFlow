This is visual acceptance only; capture process completion, source hashes and exact fixture parity remain root-owned checks. I did not run a model, test, browser or capture, or edit production source.

The visible contraction/expansion trend is improved: contraction gives a deeper bell relative to its width, while expansion gives a broad, flatter bell. These stills establish the extrema and midpoint appearance, not the temporal smoothness of the full animation or any hydrodynamic accuracy.

Close side views retain the baseline's stepped horizontal transparency bands near the crown, particularly on its upper-right side. The taller contracted profile makes those existing bands easier to notice, but I saw no new holes, inverted faces, seam breaks or major opacity change. This remains a rendering limit of the current procedural transparent model. The internal pink shapes and folded oral ribbons also retain their deliberately simplified, angular appearance; this pass changes the bell's axial motion only.

Oblique views naturally layer a scenery rock behind the transparent left side of the bell. That pre-existing compositional overlap should not be mistaken for an anatomy seam or evidence of actor contact. Fine tentacles and crown banding are necessarily less distinct in the phone images. These PNGs contain the GL render, so this review makes no claim about touch controls or surrounding mobile UI.

No concrete visible blocker was found in these eighteen paired views.
