# Working team agreements (2026-09-19)

Teamwork Builder's Contract tab now supports a working proposal that others can question and revise. It replaces a three-promise completion gate, repeatable save rewards, signature lines and a prescribed consequence ladder. The `contract` navigation ID and earlier data remain intact.

Three authored examples cover observable respectful behavior, workable response timing, and support when contributions are blocked. Each has elementary, middle and high-school setups, an initial rule, its limitations, a more workable proposal and a changed circumstance with a possible adjustment. The examples do not populate the learner's draft. Their shared explanations may benefit from adult discussion for younger learners.

Seven optional fields address purpose, whose input is needed, proposed practices, access and support, responsibilities, what to do when an agreement fails, and later review. Prompts distinguish a proposal from consent, allow disagreement, consider capacity and alternatives, and avoid requiring personal explanations. Adult help does not depend on first confronting a person causing harm. The draft does not impose peer grade penalties or promise confidentiality.

Four optional review choices consider clarity, workable participation, room to question the plan, and support/review. Choices are Still to check, Needs revision and Ready to discuss. They describe the learner's own judgment, not group approval or a score. Editing any note resets the four known review choices and announces that reset; unrelated future fields remain. This avoids carrying a review of old wording onto a changed draft. Reading or changing examples does not reset the draft or its reviews.

The design is informed by [CAST's guidance on collaboration and community agreements](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/effort-persistence/collaboration/), including learner input, clear responsibilities and support, and [CASEL's framework](https://casel.org/what-is-sel/), including collaborative problem-solving, help-seeking and considering consequences. These sources inform the design; this activity and its examples are not a validated assessment or evaluated program.

Implementation and compatibility:

- `agreementDrafts[band]` stores `purpose`, `voices`, `practice`, `access`, `roles`, `repair`, `review` and `reviews`. The latter has `clear`, `workable`, `voice` and `followup` choices. `agreementExamples[band]` remembers the comparison example independently. Drafts and choices persist across grades, navigation and serialized project restoration.
- Malformed containers, fields and review values fall back to usable empty controls. Unknown draft/review properties survive edits; only the four known review choices reset when notes change.
- Historical `contractAgreements`, `contractRoles`, `contractComms`, `contractConsequence`, `contractSaved`, awards and logs remain unchanged. Recognized entries appear in a separate history panel with a disclaimer; they are not auto-migrated into a proposal. Progress/badge wording identifies earlier records.
- The labeled read-only proposal preview includes only learner notes and current review choices, with explicit open sections and draft status. It supports native selection and copying without sending messages. No provider calls, new saved/completed flags, practice logs, XP or badges come from this activity.
- Native disclosures reduce the amount shown at once. Visible labels, described help, 16px inputs, controls at least 44px tall and explicit light/dark/high-contrast colors support phone use. Full example headings remain visible when long native selector options truncate.
- Notes belong to the current project; existing hub save/export controls keep them beyond the session. No new local-storage mechanism, deployment or packaged build is included.
